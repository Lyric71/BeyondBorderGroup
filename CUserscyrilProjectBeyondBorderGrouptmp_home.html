About us

Here's why brands work with us

The team behind BBG spent years inside Publicis Shanghai and Fortune 500 companies operating in China.

Running brand strategy, buying media, managing eCommerce operations, building social commerce channels on Douyin, WeChat and RED, localising products for the Chinese consumer. Advising brands on market entry, from business model to distributor selection to regulatory setup. Producing content at scale, both traditional studio work and AI-powered production.

We do it on the platforms, with the budgets, with the pressure. Advising from the outside is not our model.

We went independent in 2023 because we wanted strategy and execution under one roof, with the same senior people handling both. The experience didn't change. The structure did.
