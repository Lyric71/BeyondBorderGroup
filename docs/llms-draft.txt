# Beyond Border Group

> Beyond Border Group (BBG) is a B2B marketing and eCommerce agency that helps global brands enter, grow, and win in China. Founded by Cyril Drouin (former CEO of Publicis China), the agency operates from Shanghai, Hong Kong, and Paris. Services span market entry consulting, cross-border eCommerce setup on Tmall Global and JD Worldwide, distribution, branding and localisation, social commerce on Douyin/WeChat/RED, KOL and influencer management, paid media, content production (Hub Studio), and China intelligence programmes. The agency also operates BeyondCompass, a vetted database of China distributors and trade partners.
