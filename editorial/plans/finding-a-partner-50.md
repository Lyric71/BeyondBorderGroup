**What Chinese sources have that English ones do not**

- The service TP vs buy-out TP split (代运营 vs 经销), which decides who sets your price.
- Official rating systems: Tmall Global TP stars (a 12-point quarterly score, 3★ to 5★, only 4★ and 5★ meet brands at recruitment events), Tmall's v-partner six-star list, Douyin's Diamond, Gold and Silver providers (1,500 registered, 350 rated).
- Real fee structures: base fee plus GMV commission in about 80% of Tmall deals and 70% of Douyin deals; Douyin commission ladders (10%, 15%, 20% by GMV band); commission-only deals at 15% to 30%.
- Court cases a brand can learn from: a Douyin shop lost because it sat on the operator's family licence (Hunan); a TP that deleted three best-selling links on its last day (Chongqing); a RMB 200,000 diversion penalty upheld, with 14 related cases worth over RMB 6 million in total closed through mediation (Shanghai Fengxian); exclusivity breached, double damages (Beijing); the Supreme People's Court's August 2026 typical cases on agency fake orders.
- Listed store operators (Baozun, Ruoyuchen, Lirenlizhuang and the like) are competitors: their filings were researched for background only and are never cited (rule added September 24, 2026).
- Regulatory news with a partner consequence: NMPA Announcement No. 70 (July 29, 2026) lets a cosmetics brand replace its domestic responsible person without the old partner's consent; Douyin's 2026 dual-verification rule for cross-border brands; the pet-food tariff move from 4% to 10% (Jan 2025).

**Formats that win:** cost pages with sourced tables, red-flag and question lists, "X vs Y" decisions, downloadable checklists and templates (none exist in this niche), glossaries, and court-case explainers.

**Seasonal hooks:** Autumn Food & Drinks Fair (Nanjing, Oct 15 to 17, 2026), Canton Fair (Oct 15 to Nov 4), CIIE (Nov 5 to 10), FHC and ProWine (Nov 10 to 12), Double 11, CNY (Feb 6, 2027), 38 Festival, spring Food & Drinks Fair (Chengdu, late March, TBC), CBE (May 12 to 14), SIAL China (May 18 to 20), 618, Hi&Fi Asia (Jun 22 to 24).

## 2. Editorial line

TheChinaPath writes the buy-side view: what a foreign brand should check, pay,
sign and keep when it hires a China partner, with the Chinese-source detail
(fees, ratings, court cases, filings) that English competitors do not read.
Every piece ends on the Compass shortlist.

What we do not publish in this cluster: lists that name and rank partners
(Compass stays private), prices for our own services, KOL seeding as a service
(TheRedScroll territory).

## 3. Rules for every piece in the cluster

- **Tags.** `Finding a partner` (lists it on the hub, in the header menu and in the guides' Keep reading strip) plus one or more topic tags that drive the hub's filter chips: `Distributors`, `Tmall Partners`, `Douyin Partners`, `Contracts`, `Due diligence`, `Managing a partner`.
- **Links.** Up to the guide it serves, to `/compass`, and across to one other cluster piece. CTA: `/compass/shortlist`.
- **Shape.** Anchor spec (answer in the first 60 words, one real table, one proprietary number, byline TheChinaPath). Explainers run 1,300 to 1,800 words, case notes 1,000 to 1,300, assets are printable pages under `/guides/`.
- **Sources.** Chinese first, every figure validated twice (house rule). Agency blogs (da-mai, xinshawn, Shanghai Jungle) can frame a range but never carry a figure alone.
- **Proprietary number.** From, in order: a Compass snapshot (section 6), the calculators, the case pages. Never invented.

## 6. The Compass snapshot (needed before wave 1 ships)

Most pieces use a Compass figure. None exists in `sources/verified-sources.md`
today, and the pipeline must not invent one. Proposal: one pull from the
Compass database into `editorial/sources/compass-stats.md`, refreshed each
quarter, with the pull date as the citation ("from Compass, September 2026").
Fields that cover the fifteen wave-1 pieces:

- partners on file by type (distributor, importer, TP, DP) and by category
- where each was first met (fair, referral, platform event)
- share of candidates dropped at each vetting step (licence, court record, references, site visit)
- TP and DP rating mix, and TP model split (service vs buy-out)
- share of TPs that open stores in the brand's own entity
- share of distributors that share scan or sell-out data, and that have a written price policy
- typical first-order payment terms
- median weeks from brief to shortlist, and months from first meeting to signature

A piece whose figure is missing ships with the next source in line
(calculator data, then a case page), per the house rule, and the log says so.
