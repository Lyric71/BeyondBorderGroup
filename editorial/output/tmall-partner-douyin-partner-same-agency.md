# Can the same agency be your Tmall Partner and Douyin Partner?

Yes, when the agency has a separate live-room team with its own Douyin
results in your category. Running a Tmall Global store and running a Douyin
live room are different trades. One contract is fine, but judge the Douyin
side on its own evidence: the people who would run your live room and the
stores they already sell for.

*Douyin's rules and the pricing regulation checked October 11, 2026.*

<!-- INTRODUCTION -->

Your Tmall Partner (TP), the agency that runs your store on Tmall Global,
Alibaba's cross-border marketplace, says it can run Douyin for you too. A Douyin Partner (DP) does
that job on Douyin, ByteDance's short-video app: the shop, the live room, the
ads and the customer service desk.

One agency for both sounds tidy: one contact and one monthly report. It can
also save real money, because the fixed fees are large.

> At default settings, a Tmall Partner costs 770,000 RMB a year (35,000 RMB
> a month plus 10% of 3.5 million RMB in sales) and a Douyin Partner 460,000
> RMB (30,000 RMB a month plus 5% of 2 million RMB). Together: 1.23 million
> RMB, of which 780,000 RMB is retainers.
> Source: TheChinaPath calculator data, October 2026.

So the offer deserves a fair hearing. The test is whether the Douyin half
of the agency exists yet.

<!-- SECTION: Why the question comes up now -->

## Why Tmall agencies are pitching Douyin now

On Douyin, brands' own live rooms now carry about half of livestream
sales. Somebody has to staff those rooms, and Tmall-first agencies want the
work.

> During the 618 festival in 2025, half of the sales Douyin livestreams
> drove came from brands' own store broadcasts, and nearly 70% of merchants
> with more than 10 million RMB of livestream sales used them.
> Source: 21st Century Business Herald (21世纪经济报道), January 2026.
> https://www.21jingji.com/article/20260130/herald/50ea314a64d44c9664cf2c6a0132a9cc.html

Douyin makes room for those operators. Its program for operating agencies
works by invitation, and a Tmall five-star operator rating counts as
supporting proof in the application. Then the bar goes up.

> Douyin's operating agencies need more than 20 staff, more than 12 of them
> on a Douyin e-commerce team, an office of at least 100 square meters and
> a 100,000 RMB deposit. An agency serving mainly cross-border stores must
> ship at least 500,000 RMB a quarter through the stores it is bound to.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), operating service provider rules,
> July 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/article/aJ2D2SbD5GWe

That quarterly bar is about to quadruple.

> A revision posted October 7, 2026, and due in force October 15 puts
> agencies whose main business is luxury or cross-border at 2 million RMB
> of shipped sales a quarter. Team size and the deposit stay the same.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), notice revising the operating
> service provider rules, October 2026.
> https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/article/aJr9FiM4w6mc

A Tmall rating won't tell you much about any of this. Tmall Global scores
its partners mostly on store size.

> Tmall Global's 2026 partner star list weights store scale and growth at
> 77%, service experience at 20% and content and innovation at 3%.
> Source: Tmall Global service provider market (天猫国际服务市场), FY26
> star list, April 2026.
> https://globaltp.tmall.com/import_tp/TP/informationDetail?id=821

Content is 3% of a Tmall star. On Douyin, it's most of the job.

## Price conflict between the two stores

Douyin sells through live rooms, and live rooms run on offers: a live-only
price, a gift with purchase, a coupon that ends with the
broadcast. A Tmall Global store holds the everyday price a shopper finds by
searching for your brand.

Shoppers see both prices, and the customer service desk on the dearer store
hears about it.

And the law now keeps the platforms out of it.

> A merchant selling on several platforms sets its own prices. A platform
> may not force it to price no higher there than on other channels, or to
> switch on automatic price-matching, through fees, deposits, traffic
> limits, search demotion or delisting. In force April 10, 2026.
> Source: National Development and Reform Commission, State
> Administration for Market Regulation and Cyberspace Administration of
> China (国家发展改革委、市场监管总局、国家网信办), Internet platform
> pricing rule, December 2025.
> https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-12/20/c_1767870953332182.htm

That leaves the price gap between your two stores as a decision you make,
or one your agencies make for you. Two agencies on commission will each
push their own channel, and the cheaper store tends to win the comparison.
One agency removes the fight, but it also controls both prices. If it earns
10% on Tmall sales and 5% on Douyin, you'd want to know which store it is
steering shoppers toward.

The fix is the same either way. Write a price ladder per product and per
channel (list, everyday, festival, live-only floor) into the contract, and
read both stores against it each month.

<!-- SECTION: Checking the Douyin team -->

## How to check the Douyin team

Ask for what Douyin asks for. Under the July rules, its admission file
includes a stamped organization chart, the staff roster, proof that staff
are on the company's social insurance, and at least two selling cases
covering live rooms, short video, creator sales and the shop. An agency that
sends those to Douyin can send them to you.

Then go further.

**Names on the Douyin team.** Ask who would host your live room and who
would buy its paid traffic. If those names also appear on the Tmall team,
you're buying one team stretched over two jobs.

**Douyin client stores in your category.** A store counts toward an
agency's Douyin record only when it is bound to the agency through an order
in Douyin's service market. Ask for the bound stores, then open their live
rooms. Douyin files padding under false transactions.

> Douyin's operating-agency code counts binding stores the agency doesn't
> actually serve, to pass assessments or ratings, as a bottom-line
> violation: 120 points at once, which means removal and loss of the whole
> deposit.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), operating service provider
> management code, July 2026.
> https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/articlev0/aJ5N3Pxir67C

Check the category, too. Douyin's service market lists brand live-room
service as its own category, apart from full brand operations, and keeps
cross-border operations in a third, for agencies serving Douyin Global, the
platform's cross-border storefront. Ask which categories the agency holds.

And sit in the studio for one live broadcast before you sign.

Our guide to [Douyin partner ratings](/insights/tmall-partner-rating-douyin-service-provider)
explains the Diamond, Gold and Silver badges and what they leave out.

## How to write the contract, for one agency or two

**One agency.** Sign a master agreement with two schedules, one per store.
Each schedule names its own team and its own KPIs, and each can be ended on
its own notice. That way a weak Douyin year doesn't force you to move a
Tmall store that works.

Give each store its own access. On Tmall, the agency should work through
sub-accounts under your main account.

> Taobao and Tmall sellers can set sub-accounts for third-party service
> providers, grant permissions, query operation logs and switch the
> accounts off.
> Source: Taobao (China) Software Co. (淘宝（中国）软件有限公司),
> sub-account license, June 2023.
> https://terms.alicdn.com/legal-agreement/terms/suit_bu1_tmall/suit_bu1_tmall201802012028_72666.html

On Douyin, the agency binds to your store through a service-market order,
and some take their commission through the platform's own revenue split.
Douyin wants the scope of that split spelled out.

> Douyin treats it as a bottom-line violation when an agency describes its
> commission as covering specified product links while the real split
> covers the whole store, every live stream, every short video or every
> product card.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), operating service provider
> management code, July 2026.
> https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/articlev0/aJ5N3Pxir67C

Name the scope in your contract too: which product links, and whether
creators' sales count.

**Two agencies.** Put the same price ladder in both contracts. Name one of
them as owner of the festival calendar, and hold one joint call a month
with both in the room. Neither agency should get the other's channel by
default when a contract ends.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is one agency for Tmall and Douyin cheaper?**

It can be. At our calculators' default settings, two agencies cost 65,000
RMB a month in retainers before any commission. A combined deal saves money
only if it cuts that figure and applies a Douyin-level commission to Douyin
sales. If the agency charges its Tmall rate across both stores, 2 million
RMB of Douyin sales costs 100,000 RMB more.

**Can a Tmall Partner become an approved Douyin agency?**

Yes, by Douyin's invitation. A Tmall five-star operator rating counts as
supporting proof. The agency still needs more than 12 people on a Douyin
team and a 100,000 RMB deposit. After admission comes a quarterly sales
bar, which a revision due October 15, 2026, sets at 2 million RMB for
agencies serving mainly cross-border stores.

**Does Douyin require my Tmall price to match?**

No platform can force that now. Under China's internet platform pricing
rule, in force since April 10, 2026, a merchant on several platforms sets
its own prices, and a platform may not punish it with fees or lost traffic
for pricing higher there than elsewhere. Set the ladder yourself and write
it into the contract.

**What if the Douyin side underperforms?**

That's why each store needs its own schedule and its own exit. With
separate notice periods, you can move Douyin to a specialist DP and keep the
Tmall team. Before you sign, write a handover clause for the shop and for
the Douyin accounts behind its live room and its ads.
