# Douyin livestream agency or Douyin Partner: who does what

A livestream agency, usually an MCN, sells a creator's time and is paid
from the commission on that creator's sales. A Douyin Partner runs your shop
and your own live room, with your ad account in the same budget. Brands that
hire the first and expect the second pay slot fees and end up with no
audience of their own.

<!-- INTRODUCTION -->

Douyin is China's short-video app, and it sells through live rooms, short
videos and a storefront. When foreign brands weigh a Douyin livestream
agency vs a DP (Douyin Partner), they are comparing two businesses that
Douyin itself files in separate systems, with separate ways of getting paid.

There is a third kind in between: an agency that runs sessions in your own
live room for an hourly fee.

## Douyin livestream agency vs DP, side by side

The two main bills sit side by side in our Douyin cost calculator. At its
defaults, RMB 2 million of first-year sales with creators moving 80% of it,
a DP on a RMB 30,000 monthly retainer plus 5% costs RMB 460,000 a year, 23%
of sales. Creator commission at 20% adds RMB 320,000, or 16%
(TheChinaPath calculator data, October 2026). That's about US$64,800 and
US$45,100 at our planning rate of RMB 7.10 to the dollar. Every third-party
figure below was checked on October 19, 2026.

## What each one runs, in Douyin's own rules

Douyin keeps creator agencies and store operators in separate systems.

MCNs sign up on Buyin (巨量百应), the back office creators use to pick
products and track commission. The bar is low.

> Douyin admits an MCN that has been in business at least six months, has
> registered capital of RMB 500,000 or more and binds at least three
> e-commerce creators.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), Buyin MCN management code, revised June 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/articlev0/114051

Store operators go through Douyin's service market instead, and the bar is
much higher.

> Douyin admits operating service providers by invitation, after a site
> visit. A provider needs 20 or more staff, 12 of them on a Douyin team,
> an office of at least 100 square meters and a RMB 100,000 deposit. Brand
> providers must ship RMB 3 million of sales a quarter through the stores
> they serve (RMB 2 million in luxury cross-border).
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), operating service provider cooperation rules, October 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/article/aJ2D2SbD5GWe

Inside that market Douyin lists full brand service and brand livestream
service as separate categories, next to a revenue-share category.

> Douyin's service market splits brand operating providers into full brand
> service (品牌全案服务), brand livestream service (品牌直播服务) and
> revenue-share settlement (分成结算).
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), operating service provider management code, July 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/articlev0/aJ5N3Pxir67C

In Douyin's books an MCN is a talent business. Some are very good at moving
stock through their creators. None of that work touches your shop.

Your own live room is another thing altogether. Douyin binds each shop to
one Douyin account, the shop's official account, and that's where a store
broadcast (the brand selling on its own account) runs.

> Douyin binds every shop to a single Douyin account, which serves as the
> shop's official account.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), shop official account notice, September 2022. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/article/aHbHwLQPDWaZ

## How each one gets paid

An MCN gets paid from the creator commission you set on each product, and
only on orders that stick.

> Commission is set at 5% to 50% on plans open to all creators and up to
> 80% on some plans for named creators. Commission is not settled on
> orders refunded within the after-sale period.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), affiliate commission settlement rules, October 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/articlev0/112620

Douyin takes its cut first. The agency's comes out of what's left.

> Douyin deducts a technical service fee of 10% from the commission on the
> agency side. The MCN sets the split of the rest between itself and the
> creator in its own back office.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), Buyin MCN management code, revised June 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/articlev0/114051

At the calculator's defaults, creator commission works out to RMB 50 on a
RMB 250 order. If those creators all sat with MCNs, Douyin would keep RMB
32,000 of the RMB 320,000 a year. The other RMB 288,000 goes to agencies and
creators, split on terms the MCN sets and the brand doesn't see. Big hosts
charge a slot fee for airtime on top.

A live-room agency bills for time, and the quotes run wide.

> A host who had sold for major brands told Beijing Business Today that
> brands hand their own live rooms to outside agencies for big promotions.
> One agency quoted RMB 2,000 an hour and another RMB 500, and the brand
> moved sessions to the cheaper one.
> Source: Beijing Business Today (北京商报), on Beijing Daily's site, May 20, 2024. https://xinwen.bjd.com.cn/content/s664b5f05e4b00fc26b9e0c15.html

That's roughly US$70 and US$280 an hour. It's also one host's account from
2024 (it isn't a rate card), so get three dated quotes for the same hours
before you pick.

The DP charges a retainer plus a share of sales. Our calculator's default
is RMB 30,000 a month plus 5%. Ads and creator commission sit outside it,
paid by you. What the live room itself costs to run, line by line, is in our
piece on [China livestream costs for
brands](/insights/china-livestream-cost-for-brands).

## Why the creator route keeps less

The invoices are only half of it. What's left on your account afterward
matters as much.

Creator sales cost more to win, RMB for RMB, than sales from a brand's own
room.

> From mid-tier creators up, the creator cost ratio (slot fee, commission
> and ad spend over creator sales) is at least 35%. A brand's own
> broadcasts typically run more than 10 points lower: one food merchant
> put its own room at 40% to 50% and creators above 50%.
> Source: Ebrun (亿邦动力), carried by Huxiu (虎嗅), July 16, 2025. https://m.huxiu.com/article/4584498.html

Brands' own rooms now carry a big share of Douyin's livestream sales.

> During the 618 festival in 2025, half of the sales driven by Douyin
> livestreams came from brands' own store broadcasts. Nearly 70% of
> merchants with more than RMB 10 million of livestream sales used store
> broadcasts.
> Source: 21st Century Business Herald (21世纪经济报道), January 30, 2026. https://www.21jingji.com/article/20260130/herald/50ea314a64d44c9664cf2c6a0132a9cc.html

Our calculator builds that shift into its second year. It moves creators
from 80% to 60% of sales and lets sales with no ad or commission behind them
grow from 5% to 20%. On its defaults, that's RMB 600,000 of second-year
sales against RMB 100,000 in the first (TheChinaPath calculator data,
October 2026). Those are model assumptions, not results. They also assume
someone is running your own room every day. A plan built only on MCN
sessions doesn't produce that line.

## What to put in the contract

China's livestream rules, in force since February 1, 2026, reach every one
of these agencies.

> Anyone who actually runs another party's live room carries the
> live-room operator's legal duties. A host agency that works with a
> live-room operator must sign a written agreement setting each side's
> duties on host management, content, product quality checks and consumer
> protection. An agency that picks products must verify the seller's
> licenses and certificates and keep the records for at least three years.
> Source: Cyberspace Administration of China (国家网信办), carrying SAMR and CAC Order No. 117, Livestream E-commerce Supervision Measures, articles 2, 43 and 45, published December 18, 2025. https://www.cac.gov.cn/2026-01/07/c_1769516655383334.htm

Build the contract around that. Then add a few lines of your own.

- **For an MCN:** the creators by name, the commission per product, any
  slot fee booked through Douyin's cooperation order, and what happens to
  the fee if sales miss.
- **For a live-room agency:** the hours, the hosts, who writes the script,
  and confirmation that the room stays on your shop's official account.

A DP contract needs one more line, on the scope of any revenue share.
Douyin penalizes providers that promise a cut of "selected links" and then
take a share of the whole shop. That rule helps only if the scope is on
paper.

Keep the shop, the official account and the ad account in your company's
name whoever runs them. The details are in [who owns your Tmall store, Douyin
shop and customer data](/insights/tmall-partner-store-ownership).

## Running a DP and creators together

The calculator's default plan is built that way: a DP on the shop and the
room, with creators booked around it for launches and festivals. The DP usually books
those creators. Ask it to report creator cost apart from its own fee, per
creator and per kept order (an order the buyer doesn't return).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is an MCN the same as a Douyin Partner?

No. Douyin registers them in different systems. An MCN signs creators on
Buyin and is paid out of creator commission. A Douyin Partner is admitted to
Douyin's service market by invitation, with at least 20 staff and a RMB
100,000 deposit, and runs a brand's shop, live room and ads for a retainer
and a share of sales.

### Can a livestream agency run my own live room?

Yes. Some agencies run sessions in a brand's room for an hourly or
per-session fee, and some brands bring them in for big promotions. Under
China's livestream rules, whoever actually runs your room takes on the
operator's duties too. Keep the account in your company's name and the
hours, hosts and scripts in the contract.

### What does a Douyin Partner cost?

Usually a monthly retainer plus a commission on sales. The Douyin
calculator on this site defaults to RMB 30,000 a month plus 5%, or RMB 460,000 a year on RMB 2 million
of sales, 23% of the total. Ads on Qianchuan, Douyin's ad platform, and creator commission are
paid on top. At the calculator's defaults those two come to RMB 440,000,
close to the DP's own fee.

### Should a new brand start with creators or its own room?

Plan for both if the budget allows. Creators bring reach fast. Their
audience, though, stays on their accounts. Your own room builds followers and sales
on your shop's account, and its costs run lower against sales: Ebrun
reported own-room cost ratios at least 10 points below creator sessions.
