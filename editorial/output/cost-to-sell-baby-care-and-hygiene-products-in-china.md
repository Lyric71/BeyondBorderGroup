# What it costs to sell baby care and hygiene products in China

Start on Tmall Global. A store for diapers and baby wash costs 80,000 RMB
there before the first sale, against 35,500 RMB on JD Worldwide and 5,000
RMB on Douyin cross-border. Commission runs 2% or 3% a sale, the bottom of
the range in our calculators. What decides the year is how often a parent
reorders.

<!-- INTRODUCTION -->

The fee sheet first. Tmall Global and JD Worldwide charge diapers and baby
wash the same 2%. Douyin charges 3% on the mother-and-baby goods its
schedule doesn't single out.

<div class="table-scroll">

| Baby care and diapers, first year (RMB) | Tmall Global | JD Worldwide | Douyin cross-border |
|---|---|---|---|
| Security deposit, refundable | 50,000 | 35,500 (US$5,000) | 5,000 |
| Annual platform fee | 30,000 | none | none |
| Commission on each sale | 2% | 2% | 3% |
| Other fee on each order | 1% payment fee | 0.9% transaction fee | 3.9% on Douyin Mall and Doubao orders |
| Cash in before the first sale | 80,000 | 35,500 | 5,000 |

</div>

> Deposit, annual fee and commission for diapers and baby care on Tmall
> Global, JD Worldwide and Douyin cross-border.
> Source: TheChinaPath calculator data, October 2026.

JD quotes its deposit in dollars, and for mother-and-baby goods the
US$5,000 holds at every sales tier. Douyin's deposit falls to 500 or 2,000
RMB after a month under 100,000 RMB of sales. A brand whose Chinese
trademark is still pending, or a multi-brand store, pays 100,000 RMB to
Tmall Global. Rates checked against each platform's rule pages on October 18,
2026.

## Where the baby care fees bite

Two lines in that table need a closer read.

Tmall Global puts diapers and baby wash in its lower annual-fee band. That
also leaves them outside this year's new-store waiver, which only touches
the 60,000 RMB band. Toys and children's clothing get the waiver. Diapers
don't.

> New stores launched between April 1, 2026 and March 31, 2027 pay no annual
> fee where their main category sits in the 60,000 RMB band; the
> mother-and-baby lines named are toys and children's clothing and shoes.
> Source: Tmall Global (天猫国际), July 2026.
> https://rule.tmall.hk/?type=detail&ruleId=20010440&cId=625

And Douyin's 3% is a base rate. An order that comes through the Douyin Mall
app or ByteDance's Doubao app pays a 3.9% supplement on top, so a box of
diapers found in the mall tab costs the brand 6.9% in platform fees.

> Mother-and-baby goods outside the named lines pay a 3% base technical
> service fee and a 3.9% supplement on orders from specified channels.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), July 2026.
> https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/articlev0/aHMYyFoqESKn

## The route into China for baby care products

Two doors. The cross-border one is open for the whole basket.

Diapers have their own line on the cross-border retail import list, with no
bonded-only remark, so they can ship from a bonded warehouse or by direct
mail. Baby wash sits on it under liquid skin cleansers, next to the
skincare and shampoo lines.

> Line 1410, tariff code 96190011, covers 婴儿尿布及尿裤 (baby diapers and
> pull-ups).
> Source: Ministry of Finance and 12 other bodies, cross-border retail
> import list (2019 edition), December 2019, as published by MOFCOM.
> https://images.mofcom.gov.cn/cws/202001/20200110143527533.pdf

On that route the goods count as imports for personal use.

> 对跨境电商零售进口商品按个人自用进境物品监管，不执行有关商品首次进口许可
> 批件、注册或备案要求。 Cross-border retail imports are supervised as goods
> for personal use, so first-import permit, registration and filing
> requirements do not apply.
> Source: MOFCOM and five other bodies (商财发〔2018〕486号), November 2018.
> https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2018-12/31/content_5437823.htm

So the baby lotion needs no NMPA filing, and the pack can ship without a
Chinese label. The same notice puts a risk warning on the order page
instead: the goods meet their home country's standards, which may differ
from China's, and may carry no Chinese label.

General trade is where a baby product and an adult one go separate ways. Take
a lotion a brand sells to adults as a general cosmetic. Put a child on the
pack, or the words "for the whole family", and it becomes a children's
cosmetic.

> 本规定所称儿童化妆品，是指适用于年龄在12岁以下（含12岁）儿童，具有清洁、
> 保湿、爽身、防晒等功效的化妆品。 Children's cosmetics are products for
> children aged 12 and under. Products labeled for the whole population or
> the whole family, or hinting at children through a trademark, picture,
> pun or pack shape, are managed as children's cosmetics.
> Source: NMPA (国家药监局), 儿童化妆品监督管理规定, Announcement 2021 No.
> 123, September 2021, via the Fujian Provincial Medical Products
> Administration.
> https://yjj.scjgj.fujian.gov.cn/hzp/flfg/202111/t20211103_5764150.htm

Once it is one, the same regulation changes the product:

- The pack shows the children's cosmetics mark, a small gold shield
  (小金盾), on its display face, plus a warning to use it under adult
  supervision.
- The formula can't use ingredients meant for whitening, acne, hair
  removal, deodorizing, dandruff, hair loss, dyeing or perming.
- The safety assessment is built on a child's physiology, with toxicology
  tests where needed.
- The label can't say "food grade" or "edible", and an online store shows
  the gold shield on the product page.

That's a lot of paperwork for a bottle of lotion.

The animal-testing exemption is shut to them as well. Since 2021 an
imported general cosmetic can skip the toxicology report under conditions.
Baby products can't.

> An imported general cosmetic may skip the toxicology test report where the
> manufacturer holds a quality management system certificate from its home
> regulator and the safety assessment confirms the product is safe.
> Excluded: products claimed for infants and children, among others.
> Source: NMPA (国家药监局) Announcement 2021 No. 32, 化妆品注册备案资料管理
> 规定, in force May 1, 2021.
> https://yjj.scjgj.fujian.gov.cn/hzp/flfg/202106/t20210608_5616054.htm

A baby sunscreen goes further still.

> 用于染发、烫发、祛斑美白、防晒、防脱发的化妆品以及宣称新功效的化妆品为
> 特殊化妆品。 Sun protection makes a cosmetic special, and a special
> cosmetic needs NMPA registration before import, where a general one needs
> a filing.
> Source: State Council Gazette (国务院公报), 化妆品监督管理条例, Decree 727,
> June 2020. https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2020/content_5525087.htm

The filing and registration steps are on our [facial skincare cost
page](/insights/cost-to-sell-facial-skincare-in-china).

Diapers and wipes follow a separate rulebook, and the cosmetics regulator
isn't in it.

> China's diaper rules are GB/T 28004.1-2021, a recommended standard, and two
> mandatory ones, GB 15979-2024 on disposable hygiene products and GB
> 43631-2023 on children's paper goods. None sets a limit for formamide.
> Source: Beijing Business Today (北京商报), June 2026.
> https://xinwen.bjd.com.cn/content/s6a395e37e4b03fa51a804136.html

One of the two was written for children.

> GB 43631-2023 took effect on January 1, 2025. It covers paper goods made
> or sold in China for children aged 6 and under, baby diapers and wipes
> included, with limits on heavy metals, formaldehyde and phthalates among
> others.
> Source: China Paper Network (纸业观察网), July 2024.
> https://www.paper.com.cn/news/daynews/2024/240726094901770836.htm

This year showed who enforces it. After self-media posts in June claimed
formamide in several diaper brands, four regulators tested every domestic
maker.

> The joint team sampled all 168 domestic infant diaper makers, 180 brands
> and 368 batches, and found no formamide. Inspected plants met the hygiene
> code for disinfection-product manufacturers.
> Source: State Administration for Market Regulation (市场监管总局),
> October 2026.
> https://www.samr.gov.cn/xw/zj/art/2026/art_e87254f9f3e8478d846182eb08fc2b63.html

The sweep covered Chinese plants and the notice says nothing about imports.
Parents read the headlines anyway. Expect the question in customer service
chat and keep your latest third-party test report ready to send.

Under general trade a diaper pack also needs a Chinese label.

> Product labels must carry the product name and the maker's name and
> address in Chinese (Product Quality Law, article 27).
> Source: China National Intellectual Property Administration (国家知识产权局),
> July 2019. https://www.cnipa.gov.cn/art/2019/7/31/art_104_67810.html

None of this is legal advice. Take the SKU list to a filing agent before
you price either route.

## What baby care costs per order

Tax first, because the shopper sees it. Cross-border goods pay no duty,
and 70% of the VAT and any consumption tax.

> Cross-border retail imports pay 0% duty, with import VAT and consumption
> tax charged at 70% of the statutory amount.
> Source: Ministry of Finance (财政部), 财关税〔2016〕18号, March 2016.
> http://www.mof.gov.cn/gp/xxgkml/gss/201603/t20160324_2510682.htm

Diapers pay 9.1%, which is 70% of the 13% import VAT. Baby wash usually
does too, because China's cosmetics consumption tax only reaches high-end
products and boxed sets.

> The 15% tax on 高档化妆品 covers makeup and skincare priced at 10 RMB a
> milliliter (or gram) and above before VAT, plus cosmetic sets (成套化妆品).
> Source: Ministry of Finance and State Administration of Taxation
> (财税〔2016〕103号), September 2016.
> https://shanghai.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfw/zcfgk/xfs/201610/t427459.html

A 400ml bottle of baby wash at 80 RMB sits far under that line. A boxed
newborn gift set needs a check with your broker first.

Now the store side, on two typical baskets. The prices are our
assumptions: a four-pack of diapers at 400 RMB and a twin pack of baby
wash at 160 RMB.

> Cost per kept order for two baby care baskets on the per-order defaults
> in the Tmall Global model, at the calculator's 5% return rate.

One caveat. The calculator charges 11 RMB a parcel whatever it weighs, and
a four-pack of diapers is a big, light box. Get a real quote for it.

The first order is the wrong place to measure this category. A family that
likes a diaper buys the same one again and again. And each year brings
only so many new families.

> China recorded 7.92 million births in 2025.
> Source: National Bureau of Statistics (国家统计局), February 2026.
> https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfbhjd/202602/t20260228_1962662.html

Every one of them grows out of diapers, so a store lives on keeping the
families it wins. Model the parent. At a 40% gross margin, our assumption
for diapers, the four-pack leaves 131 RMB per kept order after store costs.

<div class="table-scroll">

| Diaper orders per parent in year one | What one parent contributes (RMB) | Parents needed to cover the 30,000 annual fee |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 131 | 230 |
| 4 | 523 | 58 |
| 8 | 1,047 | 29 |

</div>

> Our arithmetic on the cost per kept order above, at an assumed 40% gross
> margin.
> Source: TheChinaPath calculator data, October 2026.

Read the middle column as a spending cap: it's the most you can pay to win
a parent and still break even on that parent. At one order it's 131 RMB.
A parent who orders eight times is worth more than a thousand. That cap,
far more than the commission, sets how hard you can spend on Tmall
Global's search ads or a Douyin creator.

The fee itself is a small hurdle: about 230 kept orders, or roughly 92,000
RMB of sales, clears the 30,000 RMB a year. The deposit comes back when the
store closes in good standing.

## Which platform first

Tmall Global. It ties JD on commission at 2% and sits in the lower fee
band. It also runs its own diaper-rash and allergy return guarantees,
which a store attaches to its listings. Since October 15 they cap how much
of an order a buyer can open before claiming in a dispute, which our note
on [Tmall Global's diaper and allergy return
rules](/insights/tmall-global-diaper-allergy-returns) works through.

JD Worldwide flips it when the open question is whether China wants the
brand at all. Same 2% commission, 44,500 RMB less cash to get live.

Douyin goes first only for a brand that already has video selling baby
products. At 5,000 RMB it's the cheapest way in, and on mall orders, at
6.9%, the dearest per sale.

## Questions we get asked

**Do baby skincare products need NMPA approval to sell in China?**

Not on the cross-border route, where goods are supervised as personal-use
imports. Under general trade they need an NMPA filing, or a registration
for a special cosmetic such as a baby sunscreen. On that route, anything
for children aged 12 and under also falls under the 2021 children's
cosmetics rules, gold shield mark included.

**Are diapers regulated as cosmetics in China?**

Diapers and baby wipes follow two mandatory national standards instead: GB
43631-2023 for children's paper goods, in force since January 2025, and GB
15979-2024 for disposable hygiene products. China's disease-control
authority inspects diaper plants under the hygiene code for
disinfection-product makers, as it did for every domestic plant in 2026.

**How much does it cost to open a diaper store on Tmall Global?**

About 80,000 RMB before the first sale: a 50,000 RMB refundable deposit and
a 30,000 RMB annual fee, with 2% commission on each sale and a 1% payment
fee. A brand with only a pending Chinese trademark pays a 100,000 RMB
deposit instead (TheChinaPath calculator data, October 2026).
