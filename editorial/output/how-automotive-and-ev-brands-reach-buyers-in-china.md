# How automotive and EV brands actually reach buyers in China

A foreign car or EV brand can't sell a vehicle in China through Tmall Global
or any other cross-border store. Cars aren't on the import list. Buyers find
the car on Douyin, Xiaohongshu (RedNote), and the car portals, and a dealer
or brand store closes the sale. Your job is to get qualified leads into that
salesperson's WeChat, fast.

<!-- INTRODUCTION -->

So for a carmaker, China is a demand and handoff problem. The marketplace
playbook written for skincare or snacks assumes a product page and a
checkout. A car has neither. It has a research cycle measured in months, a
showroom visit, and a salesperson who works the buyer on WeChat until the
deposit lands.

The market a newcomer walks into is shrinking at home. It has also gone
electric.

> China's domestic vehicle sales were 1.701 million in August 2026, down
> 24.2% on a year earlier.
> Source: China Association of Automobile Manufacturers (中国汽车工业协会),
> September 2026. http://www.caam.org.cn/chn/4/cate_32/con_5237330.html

> New-energy vehicles took 65.7% of domestic sales in August 2026.
> Source: China News Service (中国新闻网), citing the China Association of
> Automobile Manufacturers, September 2026.
> https://www.chinanews.com.cn/cj/2026/09-11/10694386.shtml

Monthly figures divide the published annual fee by 12. Every figure was
checked against its source in October 2026, and the sources follow.

The portals are where buyers compare trims and transaction prices. Dealers pay
most of that bill, and they're paying less.

> Autohome had 77.5 million mobile daily active users in December 2025
> (QuestMobile) and sells dealer subscriptions "mainly on a fixed-fee basis,
> typically for a period of one year."
> Source: Autohome Inc., annual report on Form 20-F, April 2026.
> https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1527636/000119312526155932/athm-20251231.htm

> Seventy-nine 4S stores in Hunan expected to pay the three car portals more
> than 25 million RMB in membership fees for 2023, above 300,000 RMB a store.
> Autohome's cheapest tier was about 234,000 RMB, after it dropped the
> 162,000 RMB tier it sold in 2022. Dongchedi's membership was projected at
> about 256,000 RMB after rebates, for a store selling the same 280 cars.
> Source: National Business Daily (每日经济新闻), via Securities Times
> (证券时报), January 2023. https://www.stcn.com/article/detail/768317.html

> Autohome's leads revenue fell to 560.4 million RMB in the second quarter of
> 2026 from 732.6 million RMB, on "reduced spending from dealers" and fewer
> paying dealers.
> Source: Autohome Inc., second quarter 2026 results, August 2026.
> https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001527636/000119312526357963/d404747dex991.htm

Dongchedi is the newer portal, and its users sit inside ByteDance's wider
traffic. The trade press measures its bill per car sold.

> Dongchedi passed 10 million mobile daily active users as of August 2025
> and served more than 30,000 car dealers.
> Source: The Paper (澎湃新闻), via Sina Finance, February 2026.
> https://finance.sina.com.cn/jjxw/2026-02-27/doc-inhphcxh3621410.shtml

Douyin now produces the volume. Dealers run their own accounts and
livestreams, and the ad budget decides how many leads come back.

> Douyin had more than 30,200 active car dealer stores in 2025, a regional
> head of the joint Douyin and Dongchedi dealer business said. Data shown at
> the same event put single-day car leads on Douyin above 810,000, with one
> in six auto-industry leads coming from Douyin.
> Source: China News Service (中国新闻网), November 2025.
> https://www.sh.chinanews.com.cn/chanjing/2025-11-28/142813.shtml

RedNote works as a search engine for owner reviews. Buyers read it before
they draw up a shortlist.

> Xiaohongshu counts 230 million car-interest users, 200 million car-content
> reads a day, and more than 1 billion car searches a month, on its own data
> to October 2025.
> Source: Securities Times (证券时报), April 2026.
> https://www.stcn.com/article/detail/3753715.html

> An enterprise professional account costs 600 RMB to verify, plus a 600 RMB
> annual review.
> Source: Xiaohongshu Juguang Help Center (小红书聚光帮助中心), August 2026.
> https://ad.xiaohongshu.com/next_help/docs/195c5fe505c71b4b0335a2fe0d61d8e0

The creator posts that feed both platforms are a content job, and that work
sits with [TheRedScroll, the group's China social media agency](https://www.theredscroll.com).
The commerce question is what happens after the lead. Our
[China social hub](/social-in-china) covers how the platforms connect to a
sale.

WeChat is where the sale gets worked. The lead lands in a
salesperson's contact list and stays with that person, unless the dealer
contract says the brand sees it too. Tencent's own video feed now sends
buyers to the showroom as well.

> After interacting with car content on WeChat Channels, 28% of users went
> to a 4S store for a test drive, Tencent's advertising unit said in a 2025
> white paper.
> Source: Tencent Advertising (腾讯广告), via Sina Finance, April 2025.
> https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-04-22/doc-inetzkuu7762499.shtml

> An overseas entity pays a one-off review fee of US$99 for each WeChat
> verification application.
> Source: WeChat Open Community (微信开放社区), June 2025.
> https://developers.weixin.qq.com/community/develop/article/doc/000e64388c0f107faf739efcd66813

The 4S dealership (sales, service, parts, and surveys under one roof) is
still the backbone of car retail in China. It's churning, though.

> China had 32,432 4S dealerships at the end of 2025, 1.4% fewer than a
> year earlier. Nearly 5,000 left their brand networks during the year, and
> nearly as many joined.
> Source: China Automobile Dealers Association (中国汽车流通协会), via Sina
> Finance, March 2026.
> https://finance.sina.cn/2026-03-18/detail-inhrmayp7468550.d.html

Shows are a launch tool. They put a new model in front of the press and the
dealers you want to sign, in the same ten days.

> Auto China 2026 in Beijing drew 1.28 million visits over ten days and
> showed 1,451 vehicles, 181 of them world premieres.
> Source: Beijing Daily (北京日报), May 2026.
> https://xinwen.bjd.com.cn/content/s69fa0b7de4b0687a28951641.html

## Why there's no cross-border route for a car

China's cross-border retail import list names every product a shopper can
buy from abroad through Tmall Global, JD Worldwide, or a Douyin cross-border
store. Cars aren't on it.

> The 2019 cross-border retail import list, in force from January 1, 2020,
> runs to 1,413 tariff lines. In chapter 87 (vehicles) it names only
> bicycles, wheelchairs, and baby carriages.
> Source: Ministry of Finance (财政部) and 12 other departments, Announcement
> 2019 No. 96, December 2019.
> http://gss.mof.gov.cn/gzdt/zhengcefabu/201912/t20191227_3451448.htm

> The 2022 adjustment, in force from March 1, 2022, added 29 products. None
> of them is a vehicle.
> Source: Ministry of Finance and other departments, Announcement 2022 No. 7,
> February 2022.
> https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-02/21/content_5674854.htm

The price caps would rule a car out anyway.

> Cross-border retail imports are capped at 5,000 RMB a transaction and
> 26,000 RMB a year per buyer.
> Source: Ministry of Finance, General Administration of Customs, and State
> Taxation Administration, Caiguanshui (2018) No. 49, November 2018.
> https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2018-12/31/content_5440499.htm

So an imported car comes in through general trade. It needs China Compulsory
Certification (CCC) before it can be sold, and it pays import duty.

> China cut the import tariff on passenger cars to 15% from July 1, 2018.
> Source: Customs Tariff Commission of the State Council (国务院关税税则委员会),
> Announcement (2018) No. 3, May 2018.
> http://m.mof.gov.cn/zcfb/201805/t20180522_2903728.htm

Imports are a shrinking slice of the market. Since 2022 a foreign carmaker
can own its Chinese factory outright, and building locally takes the duty
out of the price.

> China imported 476,000 vehicles in 2025, down 32.4%, worth US$23.64
> billion.
> Source: China Association of Automobile Manufacturers, from customs data,
> via CCTV (央视网), February 2026.
> https://news.cctv.com/2026/02/04/ARTI5WxHlrO5lEbBzoUBVRKL260204.shtml

> China removed the foreign ownership cap on passenger-car manufacturing
> from January 1, 2022.
> Source: National Development and Reform Commission (国家发展和改革委员会) and
> Ministry of Commerce, foreign investment negative list, December 2021.
> https://www.ndrc.gov.cn/xwdt/xwfb/202112/t20211227_1309928_ext.html

Douyin's own store can't move the metal either.

> Douyin's car categories (deposit, full payment, prepayment) open by
> invitation only, to carmakers, authorized dealer groups, car markets, and
> large dealers. What sells is an e-voucher the buyer redeems in store
> within 90 days.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), automotive category rules, September
> 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/articlev0/aHKhRwPcmzWa

The dealer rules matter just as much. Under the Ministry of Commerce's 2017
car sales measures, a carmaker can't stop its dealers selling other brands.
It also can't sell direct to consumers inside a dealer's territory unless
the contract allows it, so write the direct-sales carve-out into the dealer
contract on day one.

> Suppliers may not restrict dealers from carrying other suppliers' goods,
> and may not sell direct to consumers in a dealer's authorized territory
> unless the contract says otherwise.
> Source: Ministry of Commerce (商务部), Measures for the Administration of
> Automobile Sales, Order 2017 No. 1, April 2017.
> https://tfs.mofcom.gov.cn/swfg/sclt/art/2017/art_2b877ff893bc4900aef9f3b37164e555.html

## What a qualified lead costs, and how long a sale takes

Lead prices are climbing on Douyin, and fake leads are getting harder to
pass off.

> One live-stream ad buyer said 10,000 RMB of Douyin ad spend brings 50 to
> 150 valid leads, 67 to 200 RMB each.
> Source: ChinaVenture (投中网), via Sina Tech, May 2026.
> https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/roll/2026-05-13/doc-inhxtqxr4627675.shtml

Since September 1, dealers on the Douyin and Dongchedi lead tools can't type a
buyer's WeChat ID in by hand, and a buyer leaving details in a live room has
to enter an SMS code.

> Dealer streamers put the average ad cost of a lead at about 70 RMB, rising
> to 500 RMB in some sessions after the September 1, 2026 rule. Dealers ask
> them to keep each valid lead under 65 RMB.
> Source: Dianchang (电厂), via Huxiu (虎嗅), September 2026.
> https://www.huxiu.com/article/4891366.html

The portals look cheap per raw lead, at least on Autohome's own math from
2019. Per car sold, the bill is higher, and Dongchedi's was set to double in
a year.

> An Autohome vice president said a 200,000 RMB annual membership brought a
> dealer an average of 7,500 leads. A 4S store finding leads on its own paid
> 200 to 300 RMB each, he said.
> Source: Jiemian (界面新闻), January 2019.
> https://www.jiemian.com/article/2803749.html

> Dongchedi's average fee per car sold was 472 RMB in 2022 and was
> projected at 912 RMB for 2023.
> Source: The Economic Observer (经济观察报), January 2023.
> http://m.eeo.com.cn/2023/0107/574117.shtml

The cycle is long. That makes speed of follow-up the cheapest lever you have.

> Chinese car buyers take an average of 2.5 months from first browsing the
> market to placing an order.
> Source: Autohome Research Institute (汽车之家研究院), March 2026.
> https://www.autohome.com.cn/news/202603/1313166.html

> Sales consultants who followed up on the day of a store visit scored 12
> points higher on satisfaction than those who waited until the next day,
> and their share of deals closed within a week was 25.6% higher.
> Source: J.D. Power China, 2025 China NEV Sales Satisfaction Study, August
> 2025.
> https://china.jdpower.com/sites/china/files/file/2025-08/China%20NEV-SSI%20CN_FV_0.pdf

## The verdict: fund the leads yourself, and own the handoff

Don't expect dealers to pay for a new brand's demand. Most can't.

> 55.7% of Chinese car dealers lost money in 2025 and 23.5% made a profit.
> Source: China Automobile Dealers Association (中国汽车流通协会), via
> National Business Daily (每日经济新闻), May 2026.
> https://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-05-28/4411257.html

Here's the order we'd plan in. Douyin lead ads and RedNote search content
come first, paid by the brand, because that's where buyers build a
shortlist. The portals follow, bought through dealers once they're signed.
A show is for a launch moment and not much else. And every dealer signs one
rule: leads get answered on WeChat the same day, with the outcome logged
where the brand can see it. Paid reach on these platforms is what
[our media team](/grow-in-china/media) buys.

Count leads the way Douyin now counts them. Since the September rule, ask
dealers and agencies for SMS-verified leads and the share that reached a
test drive. A raw form fill tells you very little.

Direct stores are an option, too, and EV makers lean on them.

> China had 26,260 outlets in independent new-energy sales networks in
> 2025. Among those that sell cars, direct and agency stores made up more
> than half, ahead of traditional franchised dealers.
> Source: China Automobile Dealers Association (中国汽车流通协会), via Sina
> Finance, March 2026.
> https://finance.sina.cn/2026-03-18/detail-inhrmayp7468550.d.html

## FAQ

### Can a foreign car brand sell on Tmall Global or a Douyin store?

No. Cars are not on the cross-border retail import list, and a car breaks
the 5,000 RMB per-order cap anyway. Douyin's car categories are invitation
only, for carmakers and authorized dealers, and they sell a voucher the
buyer redeems in store. The vehicle itself is imported through general
trade or built locally, then sold in a showroom.

### How long does a Chinese car buyer take to decide?

About 2.5 months from first browsing to order, according to Autohome's
research arm in March 2026. Once the buyer is in a salesperson's WeChat,
speed matters. J.D. Power found that consultants who follow up the same day
close a 25.6% higher share of deals within a week than those who wait a day.

### Do I need dealers, or can I sell direct?

Either can work. Direct and agency stores now make up more than half of the
outlets that sell cars in China's independent new-energy networks. If you
do sign dealers, the 2017 car sales measures stop you selling direct inside
their territory unless the contract allows it. Put that carve-out in
writing before launch.
