# Double 11 2026 checklist, 50 days out

Print this. Write a person's name next to every line, not a team name. Cross
each line off on the date shown, not in the week it is due.

Every date counts back from Wednesday, November 11, 2026.

The reasoning behind each line is in
[the Double 11 preparation checklist](/insights/double-11-preparation-checklist).

## 50 days out: Tuesday, September 22

- [ ] Bonded warehouse deposit paid. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Bonded tax prepayment funded. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Certificate of origin issued for every festival SKU. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Free-sale or market-approval document issued for every festival SKU. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Overseas circulation evidence requested from the retailer or pharmacy (unbroken video walkthrough, or platform sales screenshots). Owner: ______________
- [ ] Product claims reviewed against the platform's claim restrictions. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Category registration and platform campaign registration submitted. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Festival SKU list frozen. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Landed cost per SKU confirmed, including cross-border tax. Owner: ______________

## 40 days out: Friday, October 2

- [ ] Base price set and the supporting transaction records saved. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Discount mechanic chosen and written down. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Comparison basis for every discount drafted for display. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Bundle and gift-set prices checked against the 5,000 RMB per-order cross-border limit. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Coupon and voucher stack modelled to the worst-case price. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Margin at the worst-case price signed off by finance. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Price locked. No further changes before the sale. Owner: ______________

## 30 days out: Monday, October 12

- [ ] Stock physically received in the bonded warehouse. Owner: ______________
- [ ] SKU-level sales forecast signed. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Return rate assumption written into the forecast. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Return handling cost per parcel confirmed with the warehouse. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Safety stock held back for the top three SKUs. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Post-festival plan agreed for unsold stock, including storage cost to Chinese New Year. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Warehouse peak-day throughput confirmed in writing. Owner: ______________

## 21 days out: Wednesday, October 21

- [ ] Store page and campaign creative approved. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Livestream run sheet and scripts approved. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Presenter disclosures ready, including the continuous on-screen label if any presenter is AI generated. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Discount comparison basis visible in every livestream overlay. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Media budget committed by channel and by week. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Daily ad spend caps set. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Creator and seeding plan handed to the social agency. Owner: ______________

## 14 days out: Wednesday, October 28

- [ ] Customer service rota staffed for the peak nights. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Customer service scripts written for price, stock, delivery and returns questions. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Return and refund policy published on the store page. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Quota rules built into the bundle builder, not the service script. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Platform rule changes since registration read and logged. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Escalation contact at the platform confirmed, with a phone number. Owner: ______________
- [ ] One person named as the decision owner for the sale period. Owner: ______________

## 7 days out: Wednesday, November 4

- [ ] Week-one sell-through pulled by SKU. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Stock reallocated toward the SKUs that are actually selling. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Ad bids and daily caps reset on the new sell-through. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Slow SKUs pulled from the front page. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Warehouse shift plan for November 10 and 11 confirmed. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Customs clearance throughput checked against the forecast peak. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Cash position checked against the ad commitment. Owner: ______________

## The day: Wednesday, November 11

- [ ] Inventory checked hourly. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Ad bids and caps checked hourly. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Clearance throughput checked hourly. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Customer service queue length checked hourly. Owner: ______________
- [ ] No new creative, no new price, no new bundle. Owner: ______________

## After: Monday, November 16

- [ ] Returns received and counted against the forecast. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Unsold stock decision taken and booked. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Actual cost per kept order calculated. Owner: ______________
- [ ] Notes written for next year while they are still accurate. Owner: ______________

## Where the dates and rules on this sheet come from

> Cross-border retail import is capped at 5,000 RMB per transaction and
> 26,000 RMB per shopper per year.
> Source: Ministry of Commerce (商务部) policy database, 财关税〔2018〕49号,
> in force January 1, 2019.
> https://policy.mofcom.gov.cn/claw/clawContent.shtml?id=65769

> An operator using an AI-generated presenter must label it under the
> national rules and keep telling viewers throughout the stream that the
> figure is machine generated. The comparison basis of any discount shown
> must be displayed clearly.
> Source: State Council Gazette (国务院公报), 直播电商监督管理办法, order
> 117, in force February 1, 2026.
> https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_12666/202604/content_7065114.html

> Selling goods or services through false discounting, false markdowns or
> false price comparison is prohibited.
> Source: State Council Gazette (国务院公报),
> 明码标价和禁止价格欺诈规定, order 56, in force July 1, 2022.
> https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2022/content_5699926.htm

> Tmall Global's 全球探源计划 asks overseas brands for proof of origin,
> evidence of authorization for free sale or market approval, and visual
> evidence of genuine overseas circulation.
> Source: Global Network (环球网), September 2026.
> https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260915A091CT00
