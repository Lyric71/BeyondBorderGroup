## One factual correction to the draft

The return-drag paragraph compared 48 RMB a kept order at 61.5% against
"15 RMB at the 5% return rate the calculator uses by default". At 5%, 1,000
orders give 50 returns, 1,500 RMB of dead logistics over 950 kept orders,
about 1.6 RMB each. The 15 RMB was the kept order's own delivery cost (4 +
11), not the drag from returns, and would have failed the "a reader can check
it" promise in the same paragraph. Rewritten to "under 2 RMB", the worked
example moved from 100 orders (where 61 / 39 gives 47, not 48) to 1,000
orders (615 / 385, 47.9), and the ASSET BRIEF block and the proprietary-number
section above were corrected to match.
