- The bonded cross-border route does **not** carry pet food past plant
  registration. Pet food is feed under GACC's feed rule; Chengdu Customs
  states that cross-border pet food must come from an approved country and a
  registered plant, which matches the 486 notice's quarantine carve-out. The
  piece says so plainly (the dairy Ledger's "bonded skips registration"
  framing does not transfer).
- Pet food's cross-border tax is 6.3%, not 9.1%: pet feed sits in the 9% VAT
  band (VAT Law, MOF/STA Announcement 2026 No. 9). The calculators' 9.1%
  is a general-goods default, not a pet food claim, so no calculator was
  changed; the tool block tells readers to set 6.3%.
- Both pet food tariff lines are on the cross-border list as bonded stock
  only; direct mail is out.
- MARA product registration covers complete foods and premixes, not
  treats; JD Worldwide charges treats 5%.
- The brief was checked against all of this and holds as written (it asked
  the writer to verify these points); no correction to the master plan.
