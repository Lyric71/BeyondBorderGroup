Platform rules, Xiaohongshu rule centre (小红书电商学习中心 规则中心)
- 《小红书招商标准及入驻规范》 (revised 2024-02-22, in force 2024-02-29),
  https://school.xiaohongshu.com/rule/detail/26/817: overseas companies may
  open 旗舰店, 专卖店, 专营店, 集合店 (卖场旗舰店 by invitation), not
  普通企业店, 个体店 or 个人店; overseas registration plus a Chinese-English
  translation; legal representative by passport; overseas trademark,
  WIPO or territorial-extension proof accepted, registered marks only,
  no pure-graphic marks; 旗舰店 needs first-level exclusive authorisation,
  专卖店 and 专营店 up to three levels; non-Chinese, non-English documents
  need a stamped translation; one company runs at most 2 stores; a
  rejected application lapses after 30 days without resubmission.
- 《关于跨境商品资质核验及宣传规范的公告》 (in force 2026-07-02),
  https://school.xiaohongshu.com/rule/detail/19/119851: four documents,
  「缺一不可」: overseas registration certificate, business licence of a
  mainland affiliate or agent with import-export scope, a mainland
  entrusted company's undertaking of joint liability, customs consignee
  registration; brand checks need purchase proof from the last 12 months.
- 《小红书电商行业资质要求》 (version 2025-03-22),
  https://school.xiaohongshu.com/rule/detail/26/807: category licences
  from the home country and matching Chinese licences held by the mainland
  entrusted company; every licence valid at least 30 more days.
- 《小红书店铺类型及店铺命名规则》 (2024-07-21),
  https://school.xiaohongshu.com/rule/detail/26/823: overseas stores get the
  "海外" suffix (海外旗舰店).
- 《小红书类目保证金标准》 (revised and in force 2025-04-22),
  https://school.xiaohongshu.com/rule/detail/27/858: default cross-border
  category deposit US$400 (US$3,500 before the revision); domestic
  flagship, franchise and specialty stores 2,000 RMB. Per-category figures
  sit in a linked sheet that was not read, so copy states US$400 as the
  default only.
- 《小红书保证金管理规范》 (in force 2026-07-01),
  https://school.xiaohongshu.com/rule/detail/27/119848: floating deposit
  for overseas merchants by previous-month settled sales, US$400
  (50,000 to 100,000 RMB), US$700 (100,000 to 500,000), US$2,800 (over
  500,000); the higher of category and floating deposit applies; refund
  1 to 7 working days after the request; the deposit-free trial covers
  only personal and sole-trader stores.
- 《小红书技术服务费规则》 (revision in force 2024-03-01),
  https://school.xiaohongshu.com/rule/detail/17/2590: base fee by category
  plus a 0.7% payment-channel fee on cross-border orders (goods plus
  shipping paid); the old first-10,000-RMB monthly waiver withdrawn.
- 2025小红书基础技术服务费费率标准 (published 2026-05-05, in force
  2026-05-12), https://school.xiaohongshu.com/rule/detail/27/2585: beauty
  and skincare 4%, women's and men's apparel 5%, supplements 3%, snacks 2%.
- 《小红书店铺服务协议（仅适用于跨境商家）》 (in force 2026-04-01),
  https://school.xiaohongshu.com/rule/detail/97/119738: deposit due within
  2 working days of the agreement taking effect; merchant sets settlement
  currency and its own beneficiary bank, bears bank charges; must declare
  goods to customs truthfully; a third-party ERP must be reported.
- 《小红书商家发货管理规则》 (in force 2026-09-09),
  https://school.xiaohongshu.com/rule/detail/35/119927: bonded goods ship
  within 72 hours of payment, direct mail and personal-postal within 120.
