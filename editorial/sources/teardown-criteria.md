# Teardown selection criteria and candidates

Odd-week Thursdays run a Teardown: "How [brand] entered China, and what it
cost them". The brand is picked on the day, against the four criteria below,
and every claim in the piece is sourced or labelled an inference.

## The four criteria, all required

1. **Live storefront on a Chinese platform.** Tmall Global, Tmall, JD
   Worldwide, JD, Douyin or Xiaohongshu store, observable today. Note the
   platform, assortment size, price positioning and review counts on the day
   of writing; those are the "where they are now" section.
2. **Traceable entry date.** A press release, filing, platform announcement
   or dated store opening that fixes when they entered.
3. **Public statements or filings about the strategy.** Earnings calls,
   annual reports, interviews with a named executive, platform case studies
   published by the platform itself.
4. **No commercial relationship with the group.** Not a current or former
   client of TheChinaPath, BeyondBorderGroup, TheRedScroll or
   ChinaWebFoundry. The client list is `src/content/cases/` plus anything a
   person adds below. When in doubt, pick another brand.

A brand that fails any one criterion is not a Teardown. If no candidate
passes, the row is `skipped` with the brands checked and the criterion each
failed.

## Writing rule, repeated

"The storefront shows", "the filing states", "the platform's case study
reports". Never "they decided" or "they spent" without a source. A teardown
that guesses at numbers is a liability.
