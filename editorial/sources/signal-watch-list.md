# Signal watch list

Monday's Signal comes from here. The rule: no platform news without an
operating consequence. A change that does not move a number for a foreign
brand selling in China is a newsletter link, not a Signal; set the row to
`skipped` and say why.

Seed list, September 4, 2026. Replace or extend it with the feeds you
actually read. Claude checks each source for changes since the previous
Monday, in this order, and stops at the first one that moves a budget line.

## Company filings and earnings (third)

Alibaba, JD.com, PDD, ByteDance disclosures where they touch fees, GMV
disclosure for festivals, cross-border segment reporting. Quarterly, so they
usually feed an Anchor rather than a Signal.

## Trade press, for confirmation only

Ebrun (亿邦动力), 36Kr (36氪), LatePost (晚点), Jiemian (界面), Caijing (财经),
Dianshangbao (电商报), Tech Planet (Tech星球). Never the source for a fee or a
rule; always the pointer to the primary document.

## What is not a Signal

- A platform's marketing announcement with no rule or fee behind it.
- A festival GMV headline with no per-brand consequence.
- A Western outlet reporting a Chinese rule second-hand. Go to the rule.
- Anything about KOL seeding, community management or Baidu SEO as a
  service (territory rule; hand off, do not write).
