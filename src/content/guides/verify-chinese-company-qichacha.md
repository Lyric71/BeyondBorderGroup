Print one sheet per candidate. Fill it in before you book the first call.
One "stop" is enough to ask a hard question. Two usually end it.

The reasoning behind each line is in
[how to verify a Chinese company on Qichacha and GSXT](/insights/verify-chinese-company-qichacha).

- Record pulled from Qichacha or Tianyancha on (date): ______________

## The eight fields

**Registration date and status (成立日期, 登记状态)**

Pass: status is 存续 or 在业 (active), and there's an annual report for
every year since founding.

Stop: 吊销 (license revoked) or 注销 (deregistered), missing annual reports,
or a set-up date that contradicts what you were told.

- [ ] Pass [ ] Stop Note: ______________________________

**Registered and paid-in capital (注册资本, 实缴资本)**

Pass: paid-in capital is on file, and any unpaid balance is due within five
years.

Stop: large subscribed capital with nothing paid in and a due date decades
away, or a GSXT flag for missing the June 30, 2027 deadline.

- [ ] Pass [ ] Stop Note: ______________________________

**Business scope (经营范围)**

Pass: the scope covers what the company will do for you, including 货物进出口
(import and export of goods) if it imports, and the permit or filing your
category needs.

Stop: no trading or sales activity in the scope, or "our partner holds the
license".

- [ ] Pass [ ] Stop Note: ______________________________

**Shareholders (股东)**

Pass: the people you met are in the registry, and ownership has been stable.

Stop: owners you've never heard of, a recent change of owner, or an equity
pledge (股权出质).

- [ ] Pass [ ] Stop Note: ______________________________

**Branches (分支机构)**

Pass: a branch or subsidiary in each city the company says it covers.

Stop: "national coverage" with nothing registered outside one city. Remember
you sign with the legal entity, never with a branch.

- [ ] Pass [ ] Stop Note: ______________________________

**Court judgments (裁判文书)**

Pass: a few cases, mostly as plaintiff collecting unpaid invoices.

Stop: repeated sale-of-goods suits (买卖合同纠纷) brought by suppliers.

- [ ] Pass [ ] Stop Case numbers to ask about: ______________________________

**Dishonest-debtor list (失信被执行人)**

Pass: no current listing, no past listing in the database's history, no
consumption restriction (限制消费) on the legal representative.

Stop: any listing, current or past, or a case closed because the court found
no assets (终结本次执行).

- [ ] Pass [ ] Stop Note: ______________________________

**Administrative penalties (行政处罚)**

Pass: none in the last three years, or minor ones outside your category.

Stop: a penalty in your category, such as expired goods or a labeling breach.

- [ ] Pass [ ] Stop Note: ______________________________

## Questions for the first call

- [ ] Scan of the business license received, credit code matches.
- [ ] Every judgment above explained, by case number.
- [ ] Actual owner named, if the registry shows someone you haven't met.
- [ ] Two brands the company carries now, with a contact at each. Called:
  [ ] brand one [ ] brand two
- [ ] Warehouse visit booked for (date): ______________

## What this sheet can't check

Beneficial ownership is filed with the authorities but not published.
Mediated disputes don't appear on China Judgements Online. Market-regulation
penalties come off GSXT after three years, or three months for small fines.
Sell-out is in nobody's registry. Ask for it.

## Where the rules on this sheet come from

> Companies file an annual report between January 1 and June 30 each year.
> One that misses two years in a row and can't be reached at its registered
> address has its business license revoked.
> Source: State Administration for Market Regulation (市场监管总局),
> 企业信息公示暂行条例, articles 8 and 18, as revised March 10, 2024.
> https://www.samr.gov.cn/xyjgs/flfg/art/2024/art_be55c2e3a54a43e5ab12794c9dc87600.html

> An LLC's shareholders must pay in their subscribed capital within five
> years of founding, and subscribed and paid-in amounts are published on GSXT.
> A branch has no legal personality.
> Source: PRC Company Law (公司法), 2023 revision, articles 13, 40 and 47,
> in force July 1, 2024.
> https://policy.mofcom.gov.cn/claw/clawContent.shtml?id=98771

> Companies registered before June 30, 2024 must bring any remaining
> subscription period down to five years by June 30, 2027.
> Source: State Council (国务院), July 2024.
> https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202407/content_6960452.htm

> A listing for obstructing or evading enforcement, or for breaking a court
> order, runs two years and can be extended by one to three. A listing is
> deleted within three working days once the debt is paid.
> Source: Supreme People's Court (最高人民法院), Provisions on Publishing the
> List of Dishonest Judgment Debtors, 2017 amendment, articles 2 and 10.
> https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/37182.html

> Market-regulation penalties that were only a public criticism or a
> lower-band fine come off GSXT after three months; all others after three
> years.
> Source: State Council Gazette (国务院公报), SAMR Order 45, article 13, in
> force September 1, 2021.
> https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2021/content_5647362.htm

> Cases closed by mediation are not published online, except where
> disclosure is needed to protect state, public or third-party interests.
> Source: Supreme People's Court (最高人民法院), 法释〔2016〕19号, article 4,
> carried by China News Service (中国新闻网), August 2016.
> https://www.chinanews.com.cn/gn/2016/08-31/7989135.shtml

> Beneficial-owner information goes to the People's Bank of China. State
> bodies and institutions doing anti-money-laundering checks may obtain it
> and must keep it confidential.
> Source: State Council Gazette (国务院公报), 受益所有人信息管理办法, in force
> November 1, 2024.
> https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2024/issue_11386/202406/content_6955755.html
