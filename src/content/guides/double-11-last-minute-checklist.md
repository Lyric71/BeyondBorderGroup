line, never a team. Tick each line on the day it is listed, not in the week
it falls.

The dates run from Wednesday, October 28 to Wednesday, November 11, 2026.
Platform dates were checked against their sources on October 17, 2026. The
reasoning behind each line is in
[the Double 11 last minute checklist](/insights/double-11-last-minute-checklist).

closed at 23:59 on October 19. Douyin's price check has run since August 31
and ends at 23:59:59 on November 11. WeChat Store's runs from September 4 to
November 15. Price rises are off the table on all of them.

## Where the dates and rules on this sheet come from

> Douyin's 2026 Double 11 registration runs to 23:59:59 on November 11, the
> sale from October 15 to November 11, and the price-check period from
> August 31 to November 11; a registered price may not exceed the lowest
> list price in that period, and listing information must stay stable
> through the event.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce Learning Center (抖音电商学习中心), September 2026.
> https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/articlev0/aJqWoaCZesUW

> Tmall's spot-goods sign-up closed at 23:59 on October 19 and spot selling
> runs from 20:00 on October 20 to 23:59 on November 13.
> Source: Ebrun (亿邦动力), September 2026.
> https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260926A07D0O00

> Tmall event goods ship within 48 hours with a carrier pickup on record and
> show a tracking update within 24 hours of pickup.
> Source: Ebrun (亿邦动力), via Sina Tech (新浪科技), September 2026.
> https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/roll/2026-09-25/doc-iniszmmx4463810.shtml

> WeChat Store's 11.11 runs from October 12 to November 15, with prices
> checked against the everyday price paid between September 4 and November
> 15.
> Source: Ebrun (亿邦动力), via Sina Tech (新浪科技), September 2026.
> https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/roll/2026-09-22/doc-inissuii2083775.shtml

> Douyin's final 双11狂欢日 phase runs from November 7 to 11.
> Source: China National Radio (央广网), October 2026.
> https://www.cnr.cn/mspd/jrhm/20261015/t20261015_527842545.shtml

> Kuaishou's 2026 Double 11 closes from November 8 to 11, followed by an
> encore window from November 12 to 15.
> Source: Ebrun (亿邦动力), September 2026.
> https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260919A088XY00

> JD self-operated phones, digital goods and large appliances carry 30 days
> of price protection after receipt; for third-party stores, the product
> page governs.
> Source: Beijing Business Today (北京商报), via 21jingji, October 2026.
> https://m.21jingji.com/article/20261013/herald/44527fe3fff27f42ec7414dc9c6a14ea.html

> Cross-border retail import is capped at 5,000 RMB per transaction and
> 26,000 RMB per shopper per year.
> Source: Ministry of Commerce (商务部) policy database, 财关税〔2018〕49号,
> in force January 1, 2019.
> https://policy.mofcom.gov.cn/claw/clawContent.shtml?id=65769

> An operator using an AI-generated presenter must label it and keep
> telling viewers throughout the stream that the figure is machine
> generated, and the comparison basis of any discount shown in a stream
> must be displayed clearly.
> Source: State Council Gazette (国务院公报), 直播电商监督管理办法, order
> 117, in force February 1, 2026.
> https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_12666/202604/content_7065114.html

> A shopper who buys online may return goods within seven days of receipt
> without giving a reason, and the seller refunds within seven days of
> receiving the return.
> Source: National People's Congress (中国人大网), Consumer Rights Protection
> Law, Article 25, January 2014.
> http://www.npc.gov.cn/c1773/c1848/c21114/c21170/c21174/201905/t20190521_210511.html

> A bonded cross-border return goes untaxed when it is filed within 30 days
> of the customs release and the goods are back at the original customs site
> within 45 days.
> Source: General Administration of Customs (海关总署), Announcement 2020 No.
> 45, March 2020.
> https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2020-03/28/content_5496571.htm
