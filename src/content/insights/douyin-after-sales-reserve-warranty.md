Since October 15, Douyin’s after-sales reserve prices warranty orders by
each store’s refund record. Our reading of the revised scope includes
Global Purchase stores. Without enough data, Douyin uses a category average
or a fixed rate capped at 1.5%. The warranty reserve holds up to RMB 50,000
or RMB 100,000 per store.

Douyin Global Purchase (抖音全球购) is the cross-border store a foreign brand
opens on Douyin from an overseas company. The after-sales reserve
(售后预留金) is money Douyin freezes out of a store’s settled payouts to
cover refunds and repairs it may owe buyers later. We read the revised
rule on October 19.

> Douyin’s revised after-sales reserve rule applies to domestic stores and
> to Douyin Global Purchase stores. It was revised and took effect on
> October 15, 2026.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), October 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/article/aJSwoqTMykft

## What Douyin’s after-sales reserve holds back

The change is in the warranty section. It covers goods in Douyin’s
three-guarantee categories (三包: repair, replacement, or refund), and
warranties a store promises on a product page or in a livestream.

> The warranty reserve covers settled, unrefunded orders still inside their
> 三包 period or the warranty the merchant promised. Douyin sets the rate by
> the store’s refund-and-compensation rate in each 三包 category and
> releases the money as warranties expire. A store without enough refund
> data is held at the category average or a fixed rate of no more than
> 1.5%. Floors are RMB 200, 500, or 1,000 per leaf category, by risk
> tier, and the cap is RMB 50,000 or 100,000 per store.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), October 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/article/aJSwoqTMykft

> Douyin’s June 2026 draft froze warranty orders at fixed rates by category
> for domestic stores, such as 0.1% on hair dryers, within a band of RMB 500
> to 50,000.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), June 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/articlev0/aJmDKphJWTr9

The October text drops that wording, and its scope line names Global
Purchase stores. Read together, a Global Purchase store that promises a
one-year warranty now falls under the warranty reserve (that’s our reading
of the text; the store’s back end shows what Douyin actually holds).

Take a foreign hair-care appliance brand on Douyin Global Purchase. Assume
RMB 1 million in settled, unrefunded sales each month and a 12-month
warranty. For this illustration, Douyin uses the fixed fallback at 1.5%.
The category-average alternative could differ; check the assigned rate.

Each month of sales stays in the pool for a year. The reserve grows until it
hits the cap.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1,000,000 | 15,000 | 15,000 | 15,000 |
| 3 | 3,000,000 | 45,000 | 45,000 | 45,000 |
| 6 | 6,000,000 | 90,000 | 50,000 | 90,000 |
| 12 | 12,000,000 | 180,000 | 50,000 | 100,000 |

The table is our arithmetic on that fixed rate and the warranty caps.
Once the store has a refund record, its own refund rate sets the rate
instead. The rule prints no ceiling for that rate, only the cap, and it
applies different rates to different stretches of the warranty.

For scale, our Douyin calculator puts the technical service fee for beauty
and personal-care devices at 4.5%, or RMB 45,000 a month at that sales
level (TheChinaPath calculator data, October 2026). A full RMB 100,000 hold
is more than two months of those fees.

> Stores with high refund rates will hold more and stores with low refund
> rates less, and stable stores with few refunds will see their holds fall.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), October 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/article/aJrPUuzZcDft

The special-category reserve for Global Purchase stores did not move.

> Douyin Global Purchase stores keep a separate reserve of 1% on sunscreen
> orders within 60 days of receipt and 6% on dietary supplements within 15
> days, each capped at RMB 100,000.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), October 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/article/aJSwoqTMykft

## Which budget line moves

Working capital.

Douyin freezes the reserve from settled funds, reducing the cash available
to withdraw. Warranties expiring reduce the hold, while new covered orders
can replenish it. Money used to meet refunds is no longer available for
release to the store.

> Douyin Global Purchase payout rules let the platform freeze a refund
> reserve out of settled payouts, based on the store’s recent refunds.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), January 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/article/aHyMoi13Bhx5

> When a store meets two or more reserve rules, Douyin freezes the higher
> amount or adds them together.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), October 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/article/aJSwoqTMykft

Track the frozen balance separately from fees and actual refunds.

## What to do, and by when

During the week of October 19, open 账户中心 > 售后预留金 > 预留规则
(account center, after-sales reserve, reserve rules) and check whether a
warranty line shows.

Before the next Double 11 session, read the product pages and livestream
script for warranty promises. A two-year promise brings the orders it
covers into the warranty pool for that period. In the fixed-rate example,
the lower cap binds in the fourth month and the higher one in the seventh.

From this month, track the store’s refund-and-compensation rate in each
warranty category as its own line. It now sets the reserve rate. If a
Douyin Partner runs the store, ask for the reserve balance on the monthly
statement, next to the payout.

Our [Douyin cost calculator](/tools/douyin-cost-calculator) prices a Douyin
Global Purchase store’s fees. The reserve isn’t among them, so add it to the
cash plan by hand.
