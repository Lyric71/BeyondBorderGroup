A branded livestream room creates a fixed monthly bill. In the worked budget
below, a daily room costs RMB 80,000 before traffic and sales-linked expenses,
about US$11,300 at RMB 7.10 per dollar. China livestream cost for brands depends
on the sales target as well as the broadcast schedule. These are budget
scenarios, not market averages or agency quotes.

Hours are on-air hours. Allow for additional paid preparation time. Hourly
figures are rounded independently, so their sum can differ from the rounded

are about US$16,100 for the daily room and US$10,300 for the part-time room. The
event is about US$29,100. No live exchange rate is implied.

## Why a project quote hides the hourly cost

A quote can cover a room, or the whole store operation. Before comparing prices,
ask the supplier to separate broadcast production from the work that continues
after the camera stops. Customer service may sit in either contract. Check the
warehouse contract for stock handling.

Get the media budget separately. A production fee tells you what it takes to
keep the room on air. It says little about how many buyers will enter. A foreign
brand needs both figures before it can approve a monthly commitment.

Our [social commerce](/grow-in-china/social-commerce) page describes the
operating scope. Use that scope to check a proposal for overlapping work,
especially if a store partner already supplies the livestream team.

## A store-led room and a hosted event buy different things

On Douyin, China’s short-video and shopping platform, a store-led room
broadcasts from the brand’s account. It gives the brand control over the selling
schedule and product presentation. The team can revise a demonstration after
reading buyer comments.

An external-host event buys access to another account’s audience. The contract
may combine a fixed placement fee with a sales commission. A multi-channel
network, or MCN, can arrange the host and manage that contract. Ask for an
itemized invoice and check for duplicate commission.

Large dedicated events can sit far outside a small brand’s test budget.

celebrity events in that interview. Replace it with the actual contract,
including what happens if the broadcast is canceled or orders are refunded.

## China livestream cost for brands, line by line

The daily room assumes an existing set in a leased space. The part-time room
assumes rental at RMB 300 an hour. Neither includes a new studio build.

> A venue supplier estimated rent for a 100-to-300-square-meter room at
> RMB 20,000 to RMB 50,000 a month in noncore first-tier-city locations,
> or RMB 150 to RMB 500 an hour. The page labels its prices estimates.
> Source: Liubai Space (留白空间), September 25, 2024.
> https://www.liubaikongjian.com/news/198.html

Those historical estimates help frame a quote request; they are not a September
2026 rate card. Get a dated local quote before choosing a lease. List the
equipment included and ask whether rehearsal time uses the same rental clock. A
low on-air rate can leave several hours of preparation outside the advertised
package.

**Host and co-host.** The daily scenario selects RMB 18,000 a month for the host
and RMB 8,000 for the co-host. The part-time scenario selects hourly rates of
RMB 400 and RMB 150 respectively.

> who put monthly pay for womenswear hosts with 1 to 2 years' experience at
> RMB 15,000 to RMB 20,000. A separate experienced host reported offers of
> about RMB 400 an hour.
> Source: Cailian Press (财联社), April 3, 2025.

This is dated interview evidence from a specific market. The co-host allowance
is our scenario input. Employer charges and any sales bonus need their own
quote; the table does not treat base pay as total employment cost.

The operator and moderator allocation is also a planning choice. Confirm the
shift coverage before assuming that a smaller broadcast calendar cuts the bill
in half. Confirm whether the host fee covers the event crew.

The platform allowance uses 2.5% from the packaged-food category in

current tariff for your product. Check the applicable cross-border category and
settlement basis before committing funds.

Orders from the Douyin Mall app and Doubao can carry a supplementary fee on top

The event assumes 25% creator commission. Store-led columns assume no external
creator commission. Neither assumption rules out employee bonuses or a partner’s
sales fee. This model applies sales-linked rates to the same sales base; actual
settlement bases may differ. Samples and giveaways use a selected 2% of sales,
valued at cost. Set a product-level allowance if that fits your operation
better.

## How paid traffic changes the monthly bill

Paid media equals the sales attributed to ads divided by return on ad spend, or
ROAS. In our store-led scenario, ads account for 40% of sales at 2.5 ROAS. That
makes media spending 16% of total sales. The event assumes a 15% paid share at
the same ROAS, giving a 6% media bill.

These inputs need testing against your own sales history. Organic sales and
creator-driven sales must also occur for the total sales target to hold. An
attractive ROAS on a small paid segment can’t prove that the whole room will
sell enough.

For the daily room, total cost equals RMB 80,000 plus 20.5% of sales. Traffic
alone matches that amount at RMB 500,000 in monthly sales under the 16%
assumption. That is a cost crossover, not a break-even sales target.

If ROAS falls to 1.5 while the paid share stays at 40%, the same RMB 500,000
sales target needs about RMB 133,333 of media. At 2.5 ROAS it needs RMB 80,000.
Keep this spending sensitivity beside the production quote.

## What each room must sell

The hourly bill helps compare suppliers. Orders decide whether you can afford
it.

Use an assumed RMB 250 basket to turn the opening budgets into orders. At the
stated sales targets, the daily room generates 668 orders and the part-time room
generates 332. The event generates 1,200. Those volumes are arithmetic
consequences of the inputs, not forecasts.

Product cost and fulfillment still have to be paid. Import tax sits outside this
room budget, as does any separate store-management contract. Add launch capital
and employer charges. Inventory funding also sits outside this budget.

Returns change the denominator. If 20% of the daily room’s orders fail to become
kept orders and its budget stays fixed, cost per kept order rises to about RMB
213.76. This is a stress test: actual refunds can also change commission and
platform charges. Reconcile those against settlement data.

At RMB 500,000 in sales, the daily room’s formula produces RMB 182,500 in cost,
or RMB 91.25 per order. At RMB 1 million it produces RMB 285,000, or RMB 71.25
per order. Total cost per broadcast hour rises because the traffic bill rises while
the schedule stays fixed.

Both formats can spread a fixed fee over more orders. An event’s placement fee
also becomes cheaper per order when sales rise; its commission remains variable.
Compare the formats at the sales each can credibly reach. Review settled orders
after refunds before expanding the schedule.

## When to delay a permanent room

Delay the lease until the margin left from sales can cover the room. There is no
universal monthly sales threshold. Work back from the margin left after
fulfillment and selling fees, then test how many orders would cover the fixed
bill. Confirm that stock will be available when the host sells it.

A brand also needs someone who can approve an offer while the China team is
working. Name that person before the first session. A host needs an answer while
the buyer is still watching.

If you are still choosing a channel, the [social selling hub](/social-in-china)
explains the platform options. Resolve store eligibility before hiring a
permanent team. A room won’t fix a product that cannot be listed through the
intended route.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a part-time room cheaper?

It can require less monthly cash. Our selected part-time budget is RMB 73,215,
against RMB 114,235 for the daily schedule. Its hourly cost is higher because
the host and space are rented by the hour. Compare total commitment and expected
kept orders before deciding which schedule to buy.

### Does a host’s fee include the whole event?

Read the scope before treating it that way. The worked event assumes the fixed
host fee includes the room and crew. It adds commission and paid traffic
separately. Your contract needs to state who funds discounts and how refunded
orders affect each fee. Have those terms written into the quote.

### Can the calculator price the room itself?

The Douyin calculator models the broader channel, including a partner’s
operating fee. It does not provide a separate studio staffing quote. Use the
room budget to inspect that contract, and add only costs outside its scope. Keep
the marketing comparison separate from the production budget until you have
removed overlapping charges.
