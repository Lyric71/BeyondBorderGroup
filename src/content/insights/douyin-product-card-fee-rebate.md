From October, Douyin’s product-card fee rebate replaces the old waiver. A
Douyin Global Purchase store pays the full category fee when a product-card
order settles, and Douyin returns most of it on the 20th of the month after
the order was paid. A skincare store now fronts 5% of those sales instead of
1%, right through Double 11.

Douyin Global Purchase (抖音全球购) is the cross-border store a foreign brand
opens on Douyin from an overseas company, usually with a Douyin Partner
running it. A product-card order starts from a product tile on the Douyin
Mall page inside the app. Sales made in a livestream, a short video, or a
creator’s shop window don’t count.

Douyin posted the updated 2026 product-card policy on October 1. We read
it on October 5.

> Product-card orders now earn a rebate after settlement, paid on the 20th
> of the month after payment. For cross-border goods Douyin keeps 1% of the
> order under the first rebate type, or 90% of its fee under the second.
> Domestic goods keep a 0.6% floor.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), October 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/articlev0/aJcQ5pzG2Vk4

Until September, the same benefit came straight off the fee at settlement.
The policy page sets the handover itself. Tasks finished in September keep
the old waiver on orders that settle in October; tasks finished in October
earn rebates.

## What the Douyin product-card fee rebate costs in cash

Start with the rate.

> Douyin Global Purchase’s base technical service fee is 5% on facial care
> and on makeup and fragrance, and 6% on apparel, for flagship stores.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), July 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/articlev0/aHMYyFoqESKn

Here’s a skincare store at three sales levels. Which rebate type it gets
depends on the monthly tasks it completes, and the store’s back end shows
which one applies.

<div class="table-scroll">

| Product-card sales in a month (RMB) | Fee taken at settlement, 5% | Douyin keeps, type one | Rebate on the 20th, type one | Rebate on the 20th, type two |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100,000 | 5,000 | 1,000 | 4,000 | 500 |
| 300,000 | 15,000 | 3,000 | 12,000 | 1,500 |
| 1,000,000 | 50,000 | 10,000 | 40,000 | 5,000 |

</div>

Over a full year, a store that clears its tasks every month ends up paying
the same. It just pays sooner and gets the money back later.

> Douyin pays most stores three days after the buyer confirms receipt, in
> categories with an after-sale period of 30 days or less.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), September 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/article/111412

> Douyin Global Purchase payouts take two more working days for payment
> processing.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), January 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/article/aHyMoi13Bhx5

So the full fee comes out of the payout a few days after the buyer confirms
receipt, and the rebate turns up on the 20th of the next month. Most
months the gap is modest. Double 11 is where it bites.

> Douyin’s Double 11 opens in October and ends November 11.
> Source: Ebrun (亿邦动力), September 2026. https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260919A088XY00

By our arithmetic, a skincare store that sells RMB 1 million through
product cards between November 1 and 11 pays RMB 50,000 in fees as those
orders settle, most of them in November. It waits until December 20 for
RMB 40,000 of that. Under the old waiver it would have paid RMB 10,000 and
been done.

## Which budget line moves

The platform fee line in the monthly cash plan. Book product-card fees at
the full category rate in the month the orders settle, and the rebate as
income on the 20th of the month after. Miss a month’s tasks and that
month’s orders stay at the full rate, as they always did.

Refunds reach further back now.

> A refund on an order already rebated is deducted from later rebates, or
> from the store’s payouts or deposit. Non-RMB deductions use the exchange
> rate on the day. Rebates may pause while the deposit is short.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), October 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/articlev0/aJcQ5pzG2Vk4

A store paid out in a foreign currency can see that clawback land at a
different rate from the one it was rebated at. On most orders that’s small
money, though it adds up in a category where returns run high.

## What to do, and by when

By October 31, check in the store’s back end that it’s registered for the
2026 policy (stores registered in 2025 roll over on their own) and that
October’s tasks are done. Those tasks decide whether October’s product-card
orders earn a rebate on November 20.

Before November 20, top up the deposit and confirm the receiving bank
account. If Douyin can’t pay a rebate for a reason on the store’s side, it
holds the money only until the last day of the following month. After
that, it’s gone.

Plan November’s cash with product-card fees at the full rate and the
Double 11 rebate landing on December 20.

If a Douyin Partner runs the store, ask for product-card rebates as their
own line on the monthly statement, read from the back end’s rebate
management page (返佣管理). The rebate lands in the store’s receiving
account, so it goes to whoever holds that account.
