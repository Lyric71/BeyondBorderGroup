From October 28, leave prices and mechanics alone. Tmall closed spot-goods
sign-up on October 19. Douyin measures every price against its lowest since
August 31, so a cut now sets your ceiling and a rise reads as a fake
discount. Spend the last fourteen days on stock, shipping clocks, customer
service and one reallocation call on November 4.

This Double 11 last minute checklist runs from Wednesday, October 28 to
Wednesday, November 11, 2026, one line a day. Each line has one task, one
owner and the point after which it can't change. Where a platform sets that
point, the table uses the platform's date. Where none does, the cut-off is
ours: the last day a change can still be checked before the peak traffic
arrives.

Cross-border stores sell inside their parent platform's calendar: Tmall
Global on Tmall's, JD Worldwide on JD's, and Douyin's Global Shop
(全球购) merchants under Douyin's rule.

Every platform date here was checked against its source on
October 17, 2026. If you are earlier than this, the 50-day version is
[the Double 11 preparation checklist](/guides/double-11-preparation-checklist).
The printable version of this one, with a line for each owner's name, is
[the Double 11 run sheet for the last fourteen days](/guides/double-11-last-minute-checklist).

## Your price is already set

The expensive mistakes in the last two weeks start with one instinct. A
product sells slowly, so somebody cuts the price. Or it sells fast, so
somebody nudges it up. Platform rules written in September now cover both
moves.

Douyin wrote its rule down in full.

> Douyin's 2026 Double 11 price-check period runs from 00:00 on August 31 to
> 23:59:59 on November 11, and a product's registered price may not be higher
> than the lowest list price the same item has carried, or is scheduled to
> carry, in that period.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce Learning Center (抖音电商学习中心), September 2026.
> https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/articlev0/aJqWoaCZesUW

In practice, a cut on October 30 becomes the new floor. You can
register more goods after it, but never above it, and the period runs to the
last minute of the sale. The same rule bans raising a price ahead of a
promotion, and it asks merchants to keep a product's specification, style,
price and stock information stable for the length of the event, with no
casual delisting or edits.

The merchant also pays for Douyin's headline mechanic.

> Douyin's 立减折扣 instant discount is 15% in every category, funded by the
> merchant, and a product can join it or the 一件直降 single-item cut, never
> both.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce Learning Center (抖音电商学习中心), September 2026.
> https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/articlev0/aJqWoaCZesUW

On a 300 RMB product, that is 45 RMB a unit out of your margin, on every
order the mechanic touches.

Tmall's door shut earlier.

> Tmall's spot-goods sign-up for Double 11 2026 closed at 23:59 on October
> 19; final payments on presale orders ran from 20:00 on October 20 to 23:59
> on October 25, and spot selling runs from 20:00 on October 20 to 23:59 on
> November 13.
> Source: Ebrun (亿邦动力), September 2026.
> https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260926A07D0O00

Tmall has also told merchants what it is watching for.

> Tmall warned merchants against price rises during the event, against
> raising a price and then cutting it, and against abnormal prices on the
> same SKU.
> Source: Ebrun (亿邦动力), via Sina Tech (新浪科技), September 2026.
> https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/roll/2026-09-25/doc-iniszmmx4463810.shtml

WeChat Store's check runs longest. Its reference period covers September 4
to November 15, four days past the eleventh, and it bans the same
raise-then-cut move.

> WeChat Store's 11.11 runs from October 12 to November 15, 35 days, and the
> platform checks prices against the everyday price paid between September 4
> and November 15.
> Source: Ebrun (亿邦动力), via Sina Tech (新浪科技), September 2026.
> https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/roll/2026-09-22/doc-inissuii2083775.shtml

Under the platform rules sits the regulator. China's promotion rules have set a
default base for a discount since 2020, and the look-back window is short.

> Where a seller does not state the base of a discount, the base is its lowest
> transaction price in the same store in the seven days before the promotion.
> Source: State Administration for Market Regulation (国家市场监督管理总局),
> Order No. 32, Article 21, November 2020.
> https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/fgs/art/2023/art_cae53a080be2401e8f91c6d6291539f8.html

> A seller may not, without good reason, mark up a list price just before a
> discount and use it as the base.
> Source: State Administration for Market Regulation (国家市场监督管理总局),
> Order No. 56, Article 17, April 2022.
> https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/fgs/art/2023/art_9a1f82a007964950a1a0f6c056f2fedf.html

JD is the odd one. It asks for no sign-up at all.

> JD kept its no-registration model for 11.11 2026: products that meet the
> rules are picked by the system, and the festival opened at 20:00 on October
> 12.
> Source: EqualOcean (亿欧网), September 2026.
> https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260925A004GR00

So on JD the price is the mechanic. Cut it, and shoppers who bought earlier
can claim the difference for as long as their price protection runs.

> JD self-operated phones, digital goods and large appliances carry 30 days of
> price protection after receipt; for third-party stores, the product page
> governs.
> Source: Beijing Business Today (北京商报), via 21jingji, October 2026.

## Stock and the shipping clock

Tmall's event rules put a timer on every order.

> Tmall event goods must offer price protection and free shipping, ship within
> 48 hours with a carrier pickup on record, and show a tracking update within
> 24 hours of pickup.
> Source: Ebrun (亿邦动力), via Sina Tech (新浪科技), September 2026.
> https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/roll/2026-09-25/doc-iniszmmx4463810.shtml

Forty-eight hours is easy in July. It is harder when the whole country ships at
once.

> From October 21 to November 11, 2025, China's postal and express companies
> collected 13.938 billion parcels, 634 million a day, or 117.8% of a normal
> day; the single-day peak of 777 million was a record.
> Source: State Post Bureau (国家邮政局), carrying Xinhua, November 2025.
> https://www.spb.gov.cn/gjyzj/2022wjmtbdbp/202511/c3af3f221f294761a50f6ebfe181e2b6.shtml

Bonded stock queues as well, and Ningbo's forecast alone runs to eight
figures.

> The Ningbo cross-border service platform forecasts about 12 million
> cross-border import declarations in Ningbo between October 20 and November
> 14, 2026, with more than 66 million items already stocked in bonded
> warehouses.
> Source: Zhejiang Online (浙江在线), October 2026.
> https://zjnews.zjol.com.cn/yc/qmt/202610/t20261016_31969295.shtml

So the warehouse confirms its peak-day throughput on November 5, in writing:
parcels per hour, and the headcount on each night shift.

Then there's the quota. Cross-border retail import caps a single
transaction.

> Cross-border retail import is capped at 5,000 RMB per transaction and
> 26,000 RMB per shopper per year.
> Source: Ministry of Commerce (商务部) policy database, 财关税〔2018〕49号,
> in force January 1, 2019.
> https://policy.mofcom.gov.cn/claw/clawContent.shtml?id=65769

A last-week gift set that stacks three hero SKUs can cross that line, and the
order fails at clearance on the busiest night of the year. Check every bundle
on November 1, while there is time to rebuild one.

And don't dump stock on the final night to clear the warehouse. The math runs
the other way.

> Our three calculators charge 0.8 RMB per unit per month for bonded
> storage. The Tmall Global calculator charges 4 RMB to pick and pack a
> parcel, 11 RMB for last-mile delivery and 15 RMB to handle a return, so a
> returned order burns 30 RMB.
> Source: TheChinaPath calculator data, October 2026.
> https://www.thechinapath.com/tools/tmall-global-setup-and-run

Three months of storage, enough to carry a unit from November 11 past
Chinese New Year on February 6, 2027, costs 2.4 RMB. One return costs as much as storing a unit for
more than three years. A deep final-night bundle that sends parcels back
empties the shelf at the worst possible price. You can run your own numbers in the
[Tmall Global setup and run calculator](/tools/tmall-global-setup-and-run).

## November 4 and the final week

The reallocation call is the one meeting in this window that changes money.
Week-two sell-through tells you which SKUs are real. Move stock and budget
toward them while the warehouse can still act, because the platforms are
about to stack their peaks on top of each other.

> Douyin's 2026 Double 11 runs its opening phase from October 15 to 18, spot
> and category days from October 19 to November 6, and its final 双11狂欢日
> phase from November 7 to 11.
> Source: China National Radio (央广网), October 2026.
> https://www.cnr.cn/mspd/jrhm/20261015/t20261015_527842545.shtml

Kuaishou's closing phase overlaps it.

> Kuaishou's 2026 Double 11 closes from November 8 to 11, followed by an
> encore window from November 12 to 15.
> Source: Ebrun (亿邦动力), September 2026.
> https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260919A088XY00

The freeze sits on November 6 for that reason. It is the last evening before
Douyin puts its traffic behind the final phase. From the seventh, the only
levers left are stock and bids, and one person (the decision owner, named
in writing) pulls them.

## After the eleventh

The returns work runs well past the sale.
The sale doesn't stop at midnight everywhere. Tmall sells to November 13,
WeChat Store and Kuaishou's encore to November 15. Keep the war room on the
platforms still selling, and keep the returns desk staffed after the others
close.

Returns have their own clock.

> A shopper who buys online may return goods within seven days of receipt
> without giving a reason, and the seller refunds within seven days of
> receiving the return.
> Source: National People's Congress (中国人大网), Consumer Rights Protection
> Law, Article 25, January 2014.
> http://www.npc.gov.cn/c1773/c1848/c21114/c21170/c21174/201905/t20190521_210511.html

Bonded returns carry a second one, and this one costs tax.

> A bonded cross-border return goes untaxed when the return is filed within
> 30 days of the customs release and the goods are back at the original
> customs site within 45 days.
> Source: General Administration of Customs (海关总署), Announcement 2020 No.
> 45, March 2020.
> https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2020-03/28/content_5496571.htm

Count it from your busiest release. An order cleared on November 11 has to be
filed by December 11 and back at the original customs site by December 26. Put both dates on
the returns desk's wall now.

## Questions we get asked

**Can we still change our Double 11 price after October 28?**

You can cut it, but the cut becomes your ceiling. Douyin's check runs to
November 11 and WeChat Store's to November 15, and both ban raise-then-cut.
On JD a cut can trigger price-protection claims from earlier buyers. Tmall's
sign-up closed October 19. Treat October 28 as the last day you touch price.

**Is it too late to join Double 11 on Douyin?**

Not formally. Douyin's registration stays open until 23:59:59 on November 11.
The catch is the price: a product can't register above the lowest list price
it has carried since August 31, and if it joins the 立减折扣 mechanic, the
merchant funds a 15% cut. Run the margin at that price before you register.

**When does Double 11 2026 actually end?**

It depends on the platform. Douyin's sale ends at 23:59:59 on November 11.
Tmall sells spot goods until 23:59 on November 13. WeChat Store and Kuaishou's
encore window run to November 15. Plan staff and stock for the platform that
closes last.
