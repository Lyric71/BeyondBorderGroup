A Douyin store is an operating commitment. Someone answers shopper chats in
under 90 seconds from 8 a.m. to 11 p.m., China time, ships bonded orders
within 48 hours, and puts up new video every week. Douyin ads and creator
posts are a budget line. Most foreign brands that open the store only wanted
the budget line.

So the Douyin store vs Douyin ads decision comes down to staffing more than
marketing. Media runs on money and a brief. Opening a store means a team
that works to the platform's deadlines seven days a week, plus a mainland
company that answers for it to customs.

The cash gap is wide. At the default settings of our Douyin calculator, a
cross-border store costs RMB 1,700,800 in its first year on RMB 2 million
of sales. RMB 405,000 of that comes back on exit. Every Douyin rule cited
below was rechecked on the platform's own pages on October 2, 2026.

> A Douyin cross-border store at RMB 2 million of year-one sales costs
> RMB 1,700,800 in year-one cash, RMB 405,000 of it refundable, and its
> running cost equals 63.5% of sales.
> Source: TheChinaPath calculator data, September 2026.

## Why brands mix them up, and what it costs

Douyin has just over a billion monthly users.

> Douyin's main app had 1,009.0 million monthly active users in March 2026.
> Source: Kuai Technology (快科技) citing QuestMobile, via Sina Tech, April 2026. https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/discovery/2026-04-29/doc-inhwckrh8029807.shtml

A brand manager watches a creator clear a product's stock in one evening
and concludes the brand needs a store. On Douyin, that's correct as far as
it goes, because a sale only happens in a Douyin store. Taobao and JD
products left the livestream cart in 2020, and the current creator rules
bar videos from steering viewers to buy anywhere else.

> From October 9, 2020, products from third-party platforms could no longer
> enter Douyin livestream carts.
> Source: Jiemian (界面新闻), August 2020. https://www.jiemian.com/article/4887329.html

> Douyin's creator rules list third-party platform links or QR codes not
> integrated by Douyin as violations, and bar content that steers users to
> browse or buy on a third-party site.
> Source: Ocean Engine Xingtu (巨量星图) help center, June 2026. https://www.xingtu.cn/help-center/author/109258

The trouble is the order. Being seen on Douyin is a media purchase.
Checkout on Douyin comes with a year of daily operations, RMB 1.7 million
of cash at our calculator's defaults, and you pay before anyone knows
whether the brand's videos convert.

## Douyin as media: what it does, what it costs, what it leaves behind

For a brand without a store, media on Douyin means creator posts bought
through Xingtu (巨量星图), the platform's creator marketplace, and feed ads
bought through Ocean Engine, ByteDance's ad system.

Xingtu's floor is low.

> A Xingtu recruitment task starts at a RMB 10,000 order budget, and the
> platform charges a 5% service fee.
> Source: Ocean Engine Xingtu (巨量星图) help center, August 2026. https://www.xingtu.cn/help-center/demander/126583

The creators are where the money goes.

> Sponsored creator posts averaged RMB 39,700 each on Douyin from January
> to October 2024.
> Source: Jiemian (界面新闻) citing QuestMobile, March 2025. https://www.jiemian.com/article/12440261.html

> Brands spent an average of RMB 324,000 a month on sponsored creator
> content on Douyin from January to July 2024.
> Source: DoNews citing QuestMobile, September 2024. https://www.donews.com/news/detail/1/4547390.html

Divide one by the other (the two periods differ by three months) and the
average brand bought about eight sponsored posts a month, roughly two a
week. Feed ads come on top. Ocean Engine publishes no dated rate card for
them, so we haven't priced them here.

Qianchuan, the tool most people mean by "Douyin ads," belongs to the store
route. Douyin opens it to merchants, agencies, and creators who sell on the
platform, and its credits only promote Douyin products and livestreams
with a cart.

> Qianchuan is open to merchants, agencies, and creators with livestream,
> short-video selling, or mall promotion needs; its promotion credits are
> used only for Douyin e-commerce products and cart-enabled livestreams,
> and a plan's daily budget defaults to a minimum of RMB 100.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce Learning Center (抖音电商学习中心), June 2025. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/articlev0/aHvyeGCvMaTB

Where does the buyer go, then? To search. A viewer who likes the video
opens Tmall or JD and types the brand name. You won't see that path order
by order. You'll see it in your Tmall Global or JD Worldwide store's search
traffic, and in comments asking where to buy.

The reach ends with the budget. The videos stay, though. Xingtu lets a
brand run a creator's original post as an ad once the creator agrees, and
that authorization doesn't expire.

> On Xingtu, a creator's authorization to boost the original video is
> long-term; an optional flat license fee runs from RMB 20 up to 50% of the
> task amount.
> Source: Ocean Engine Xingtu (巨量星图) help center, June 2026. https://www.xingtu.cn/help-center/demander/139122

The creator side can be bought from outside China. Xingtu clears an
overseas brand's paperwork within 24 hours.

> Xingtu reviews a brand's qualifications in about six hours, and an
> overseas entity's within 24 hours.
> Source: Ocean Engine Xingtu (巨量星图) help center, June 2026. https://www.xingtu.cn/help-center/demander/112557

Picking and briefing creators is seeding work. That sits with
TheRedScroll, the group's China social media agency
(https://www.theredscroll.com). The ad buying, and the numbers behind it,
are on our [China media page](/grow-in-china/media).

## The Douyin store: the daily commitment behind the storefront

A foreign brand sells on Douyin through Douyin Global (抖音全球购), the
cross-border store format. The paperwork is the easy part.

> A Douyin Global store must be opened by a company registered outside
> mainland China, with an overseas corporate bank account and a mainland
> domestic agent; a flagship store needs a home-market trademark
> certificate valid for more than six months.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), Douyin Global merchant admission rules, July 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/article/108056

Choose the domestic agent with care.

> The domestic agent registers with customs, is responsible for truthful
> declaration, and carries joint civil liability.
> Source: Ministry of Commerce (商务部) and five agencies, Shangcaifa [2018] No. 486, November 2018. https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2018-12/31/content_5437823.htm

The deposit is small and follows last month's sales.

> Douyin Global's base deposit is RMB 500 at up to RMB 50,000 of last
> month's sales, RMB 2,000 up to RMB 100,000, and RMB 5,000 above that.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), Douyin Global deposit rules, June 2025. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/articlev0/107821

Then the clocks start. Douyin times how fast a store answers chats in
Feige, its merchant messaging tool. The average reply has to land in under
90 seconds on chats opened between 8 a.m. and 11 p.m., and a message left
unanswered counts as ten minutes.

> A store's average Feige response time must be under 90 seconds, measured
> on chats opened between 8:00:00 and 22:59:59; an unanswered message
> counts as 10 minutes.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), Feige service rules, January 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/articlev0/101788

That's 15 staffed hours a day, or about 450 hours of desk time in a 30-day
month, by our arithmetic. And that's before a box is packed or a camera
goes on.

Bonded orders ship within 48 hours of payment, and lateness is charged.

> Bonded orders must ship within 48 hours of payment; late shipments are
> deducted 1% to 5% of the amount paid, with minimums of RMB 2 to RMB 5,
> and mass violations freeze 15% of the last 15 days' settlement, at least
> RMB 1,000, for up to 90 days.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), Douyin Global shipping rules, April 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/articlev0/107820

Refund requests have their own timers, and silence counts as a yes.

> Douyin gives merchants 24 hours on pre-shipment refund requests, 36 hours
> on post-shipment requests, and 48 hours after a return is signed for; on
> timeout, the system treats the merchant as agreeing.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), merchant after-sale rules, August 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/articlev0/104600

Every one of those timers feeds a merchant experience score, and a low
score shuts the store out of Qianchuan.

> Douyin Global scores stores from 0 to 100 over 30 days; service weighs
> 33% for cross-border FMCG and 55% for cross-border health, and a store
> below the threshold is barred from buying Qianchuan ads.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), Douyin Global merchant experience score rules, September 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/articlev0/aHMSpgQDfmxK

Content is the other daily load. Store livestreams, run by the brand's own
team, now outsell creator livestreams.

> Store livestreams accounted for more than 30% of Douyin e-commerce GMV in
> 2024 and outsold creator livestreams for a second year running.
> Source: Economic Information Daily (经济参考报), via Xinhua, March 2025. https://app.xinhuanet.com/news/article.html?articleId=f748ec944eff412bf8a1fb4b9c21f2f6

The cadence our [social commerce](/grow-in-china/social-commerce) team
commits to for a store is three to seven livestreams a week, with short
videos in between. Creators sell on top of that, on commission.

> Douyin creator commissions run from 5% to 50% of the order on open plans
> and up to 80% on plans set for named creators.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), affiliate settlement rules, August 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/articlev0/112620

Douyin's own cut is one of the lighter lines: a base technology fee of 2%
to 6% by category, plus a supplement on orders that come through the
Douyin Mall app and Doubao (ByteDance's AI assistant, which now sells too).

> Douyin Global's base technology service fee runs from 2% to 6% by
> category; orders from the Douyin Mall app and Doubao add a supplement,
> for example 4% on apparel and 5% on makeup.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce (抖音电商), Douyin Global fee schedule, July 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/articlev0/aHMYyFoqESKn

People cost more than the platform does. At our calculator's defaults, the
operating partner's retainer is the biggest single line at RMB 360,000 a
year, just ahead of RMB 320,000 in creator commission, and the whole store
runs at RMB 105,900 a month.

The retainer is what pays for the people who keep those deadlines.

## The three questions that decide it

**Who answers Feige at 10 p.m. on a Sunday?** Your own team in China, or
an operating partner on a retainer. Our calculator's retainers run from
about RMB 30,000 a month for a lean setup to RMB 100,000 for the
international-brand tier. If neither exists, you're buying media.

Then ask where the demand lands. A brand with a Tmall Global or JD
Worldwide store can treat Douyin as the shop window and let search finish
the sale. A brand with no store anywhere in China has to pick its first
one, and the Douyin store is one candidate next to those two.

**Does your margin carry the store?** Creators, ads, tax, and fees take a
fixed share of every sale before the margin pays for the team. So
breakeven climbs fast as the margin thins.

> At the Douyin calculator's default settings, breakeven sales are
> RMB 1.25 million a year at a 75% gross margin, RMB 2.55 million at 60%,
> and RMB 8.26 million at 50%.
> Source: TheChinaPath calculator data, September 2026.

## The sequence that works: media first, store when the numbers say so

Start with media. Buy a few creator posts through Xingtu, keep the reuse
rights, and put a modest ad budget behind the two or three videos that hold
attention. Give the test a quarter and a fixed budget, then watch your
Tmall or JD search traffic and the comments.

The store comes later. Creators start asking for a cart link, the
comments keep asking where to buy, and your sales plan clears breakeven at
your margin. Put someone on the Feige desk before launch day.

Open small. A store selling under RMB 50,000 a month posts a RMB 500
deposit, so the platform doesn't punish a slow start. The clocks don't
scale down, though. The 90-second reply target applies at ten orders a day
the same as at a thousand.

If you'd rather not hire, a vetted operating partner is the usual route.

## Questions brands ask about a Douyin store

### Can I sell on Douyin without a Douyin store?

Not through a link of your own. Livestream carts have carried only Douyin
store products since October 2020, and creator videos can't point viewers
to Tmall or JD. A creator can still sell your product from a Douyin store
authorized to carry your brand. Without any store, Douyin works as media
that sends people to search elsewhere.

### Does a foreign brand need a Chinese company to open a Douyin store?

The store itself must sit with a company outside mainland China. You also
need a mainland company as your domestic agent with customs, and it
carries joint civil liability for the declarations. In the setup our
calculator models, the operating partner or the bonded warehouse usually
takes that role inside its fees.

### How much does a Douyin store cost per month?

About RMB 105,900 a month to run at our calculator's defaults, for RMB 2
million of yearly sales. The retainer, creator commission, and
cross-border tax are the largest lines. Setup adds RMB 430,000 in the
first year, and RMB 405,000 of that is refundable deposits and tax
prepayment.

### How much do Douyin ads cost?

Creator posts are the big line: RMB 39,700 per sponsored post on average
in 2024, per QuestMobile, and RMB 324,000 a month per brand. A Xingtu task
starts at RMB 10,000. Qianchuan plans can start at RMB 100 a day, but
Qianchuan only promotes products and livestreams in a Douyin store, so it
can't send traffic to Tmall.
